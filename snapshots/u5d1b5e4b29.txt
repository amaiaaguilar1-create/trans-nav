Financial Counseling
Counselors are available at the Sidney & Lois Eskenazi Hospital, Sandra Eskenazi Mental Health Center and Eskenazi Health Center locations to help patients determine the method of payment and/or insurance plan that is best for them.
1.855.202.1053
There are many different health care coverage programs available. What do you qualify for? What's the right coverage option for you and your family? The Eskenazi Health Financial Eligibility Services team is here to help you explore your options and ultimately apply for the program that is best for you.
Eskenazi Health provides primary care through the Eskenazi Health Center system, which has multiple locations throughout Marion County. Eskenazi Health Center is a Federally Qualified Health Center and does not deny services to any individual based on inability to pay; it is open to all. All Eskenazi Health Center locations have a sliding fee schedule that bases discounts on family size and income. There is also an optical shop sliding fee schedule .
Financial counselors also can give referrals for other payor resources to qualified patients. Counselors are available at Sidney & Lois Eskenazi Hospital , Monday - Friday, 8 a.m. - 6 p.m. Please see individual Eskenazi Health facilities listed in the locations tab below for their hours.
Appointments are currently needed to meet with a financial counselor. Appointments can be made by calling 1.855.202.1053 or scheduled online through Eskenazi Health MyChart. Login to your Eskenazi Health MyChart account or visit the MyChart page to learn more. Patients in need of food assistance are encouraged to visit the Food as Medicine page to learn more about available programs including information about SNAP application assistance.
Eskenazi Health would like to remind all patients covered by Medicaid to visit FSSABenefits.IN.gov to report any changes and check eligibility. Normal redetermination processes have resumed to determine annual Medicaid eligibility. Many redeterminations are done automatically based on information the state has available. However, in some situations, the state of Indiana will need to ask the member for information about themselves and their family, such as current address, employment status and income, age, and family size. Patients with questions or concerns about eligibility redeterminations may contact a member of the Financial Eligibility Services team by calling 1.855.202.1053 or scheduling an appointment online through Eskenazi Health MyChart .
