Specialized Care
At Thrive Alabama, we recognize that patients often have unique health care needs. We are committed to providing care to these clients with understanding, compassion and respect. Our goal is to provide personalized, holistic healthcare. We offer a range of supportive services and our experienced team will all work together to deliver the care you need.
Patient Services Provided
Physical Examinations Annual checkups that include blood pressure testing and appropriate preventative lab tests
Care for Common Conditions Diagnosis and treatment of everyday illnesses
Acute Care Treatment for common infections and conditions such as asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease exacerbations, skin infections, and urinary tract infections, as well as performing other non-emergency procedures
Chronic Disease Management Management of illnesses such as diabetes, high blood pressure, arthritis, and heart conditions through an integrated care plan of checkups, screenings, and monitoring
Immunizations Shots to protect you against a variety of diseases as well as seasonal flu shots.
Cancer Screenings or referrals for breast, cervical, colon, and prostate cancers
Case Management We can help you overcome obstacles to accessing care
Translation services for your appointment
Transportation to your appointment
Affordable Care Act insurance application assistance
