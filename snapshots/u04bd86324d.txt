About La Clínica del Pueblo
Learn More About Us
La Clínica del Pueblo started in 1983 to serve the Latino community. Since then, we have grown into a full-service Community Health Center that welcomes all patients. We provide high-quality, bilingual care that respects who you are and where you come from. We serve the whole DMV area — Washington, DC, Maryland, and Virginia.
Our model brings together medical care, mental health support, a pharmacy, case management, prevention, health education, and community resources. We believe care works best when patients get help in their own language, close to home, from a team that understands them.
Why Choose La Clínica
Patients choose La Clínica because we offer more than just one visit. We help people build long-term relationships with doctors, manage health needs over time, get medicine, connect to community help, and feel respected
Bilingual care
Our team provides care and support in English and Spanish.
Whole-person support
We connect medical care with behavioral health, pharmacy, prevention, case management, and community resources.
Rooted in community
Since 1983, La Clínica has worked with Latino and immigrant communities to help more people get quality health care.
Expanding access to quality care
Every year, La Clínica helps thousands of people receive the care, information, and support they need to live healthier lives. Our work includes primary care, pediatrics, behavioral health, HIV prevention and care, pharmacy services, reproductive health, domestic violence prevention, and community resource navigation.

Health goes beyond the exam room
Through Salud Es, La Clínica connects you to trusted help outside the clinic — housing, food, jobs, income, legal help, and more.
Food & Nutrition
Access to healthy, affordable food is essential to overall health and well-being. This community resource exists to support the community in the Washington, DC area by connecting individuals and families to culturally familiar, nutritious food options and reliable food assistance programs.
Housing
Finding safe, stable, and affordable housing is a critical need. We connect community members with local housing assistance programs, rental support, emergency shelters, and resources for navigating tenant rights in the DC metropolitan area.
Employment & Income
Economic stability starts with meaningful employment and fair wages. We help community members access job training, resume assistance, workforce development programs, and financial literacy resources to build a stronger future for themselves and their families.
Legal
Navigating the legal system can be overwhelming. We provide connections to trusted legal aid organizations, know-your-rights workshops, and advocacy resources throughout the DC area.

FAQs
What services does La Clínica del Pueblo provide?
La Clínica del Pueblo offers primary care, care for kids, mental health services, a pharmacy, HIV prevention and care, LGBTQIA+ health services, sexual and reproductive health care, substance use treatment, domestic violence prevention, and help finding community resources.
Where is La Clínica del Pueblo located?
La Clínica del Pueblo serves patients across DC, Maryland and Virginia with locations in DC and Hyattsville.
Does La Clínica del Pueblo provide care in Spanish?
Yes. La Clínica provides bilingual care and support in English and Spanish.
Can I become a patient if I do not have insurance?
Yes. La Clínica welcomes patients with insurance. For uninsured patients, a discounted fee is available.
Does La Clínica del Pueblo offer pediatric care?
Yes. We care for children and teens, including checkups, shots, and support for the whole family.
Does La Clínica del Pueblo have a pharmacy?
Yes. Our pharmacy helps patients get and manage their medicine.
