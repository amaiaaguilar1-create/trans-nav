HOW TO USE THE SCHEDULE OF COURT COST
This schedule separates the fees for filing of pleadings; issuance of citations, notices and writs;
and the service of process/issuance. To determine the amount of cost, please calculate:
Applicable Filing Fee + Process Issuance Fee + Fee for Serving Process = Total Fee
