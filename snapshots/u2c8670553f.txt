Vital Records Services
How to Order Certificates
Vital Records Services helps the public file and access vital records. Vital records include birth, death, fetal death, marriage, divorce and other related data. Vital events are filed with the registrar in the county of occurrence and then are recorded with the state.
Corrections and Amendments
Vital Records Services files corrections for persons whose vital event (birth, death, or marriage) occurred in Wyoming.
Establish Paternity
Acknowledgment of paternity legally establishes the father of the child. Laws regarding Paternity can be found in the Wyoming Parentage Act W.S. 14-2-401, et seq.
Adoptions
Vital Records Services files Adoptions for persons born in Wyoming or born in a foreign country and re-adopted through Wyoming District Courts.
If your vital event occurred in another state, please visit the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) &ldquo;Where to Write&rdquo; web page to find contact information for other states and territories.
