Where to Start
You’ll want to have your fingerprints done (two sets) and complete your FBI and SBI criminal
background checks first. The report you receive from SBI and FBI must be dated within 90 days of
your Name Change petition that you submit to the court. Fingerprints are done at the Sheriff’s office
in your county and typically cost $10.00 per set. Please note: some counties require an appointment for
fingerprints- it is recommended that you call ahead to learn how your county handles fingerprints.
Once you have your fingerprint cards, you are ready to complete your SBI and FBI Background checks.
FBI Background Check
For information on FBI background checks, visit: https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/more-fbi-
services-and-information/identity-history-summary-checks. Here, you can access the Applicant
Information Form. The fee for the background check is $18.00. Notes: When it asks the reason for the
background check, select “Other.” You have the option to submit the form for your FBI background
check online or via mail. You will receive results more quickly if you choose to submit your form online.
SBI Background Check
For information on SBI background checks, visit: https://www.ncsbi.gov/Services/SBI-Forms.aspx. You
will select “Background Check for NC Criminal Records (Right to Review Process PDF).” The fee for the
background check is $14.00. Note: When it asks for the reason for the background check, write
“Summary Request.”

Original/Certified Birth Certificate
Obtain one original/certified birth certificate from NC Vital Records, if you do not have one already.
https://vitalrecords.nc.gov/
If you were born in North Carolina: You can also get a certified copy of your birth certificate from the
county Register of Deeds where you were born. To find the register of deeds, visit
https://www.ncard.us/find-your-register-of-deeds/
If you were not born in North Carolina, do a google search for the vital records office in the state where
you were born.
Once you have your SBI and FBI Background Checks in hand along with your Certified Birth Certificate,
you can proceed on with the rest of the process.
PLEASE NOTE: The FBI process can take over a month longer than the SBI background check, and the
petition of name change MUST be filed within ninety (90) days of the background checks, so it is
recommended that you receive the results of the FBI Background Check before requesting the SBI
background check.

Notice of Intent to File for Name Change
Before you submit your Petition for Adult Name change and accompanying documents, you will need
to see the Clerk of Superior Court (Special Proceedings) in the county where you live to post your
Notice of Intent to File Name Change. The clerk will tell you where the appropriate location to place the
notice is located. It will need to be posted for ten (10) consecutive days.
Petition for Name Change
When the notice period is up, you will then file your Petition for Name Change. You will need to bring
the following documents:
 Petition for Name Change*
 Notarized Verification Page*
 Two (2) Affidavits of Good Character*
 Notarized Affidavit Regarding Outstanding Tax or Child Support Obligation*
 Original/Certified Birth Certificate
 Photocopy of Birth Certificate

Copies of Court-Ordered Name Change
When the Clerk issues the name change, obtain 5 certified copies of the court-ordered name change.
The cost varies by county. Make sure the Clerk sends the pink copy of the court-ordered name change
to the NC Vital Records.
Steps for Legal Name Change After Receiving Court-Ordered Name Change
1. Change your name with the Social Security Office
a. You will need:
i. Certified Copy of Court-Ordered Name Change
ii. U.S. birth certificate or U.S. passport
iii. Driver’s license
iv. Application for Social Security Card (https://www.ssa.gov/forms/ss-5.pdf)
2. Change Name on Your Birth Certificate
a. Born in North Carolina:
i. You will need:
1. Application for a Copy of a North Carolina Birth Certificate
a. Complete the “Record Changes” section regarding “Name
Change”
2. Copy of driver’s license or passport
3. $39.00 money order or certified check
b. Born in another state:
i. Visit https://transequality.org/documents, and select your state on the map to
find more information.
3. Change Name on Driver’s License
a. You will need:
i. Certified Copy of Court-Ordered Name Change
ii. New Social Security Card
iii. Current driver’s license
Note: You must notify the NC DMV within 60 days after the name change occurs and obtain a
duplicate card with the your new name.

Once you have changed your name in these places, you may begin changing it everywhere else, for
example: post office, IRS, voter registration, banks, credit cards, cell phone plans, utilities, doctors,
insurance companies, state tax authority, clubs, memberships, employer, retirement plans, will, health
care proxy, living will, trust, contracts, power of attorney, etc.
