Communicable Disease Unit
The Wyoming Department of Health Communicable Disease Unit (CDU) manages prevention, surveillance, linkage to care, and technical assistance for HIV, chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, hepatitis B virus, hepatitis C virus, and tuberculosis in Wyoming.
