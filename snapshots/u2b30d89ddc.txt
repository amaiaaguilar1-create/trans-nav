Point of Pride provides financial aid and direct support to trans folks in need of health and wellness care.
Get support
Annual Trans Surgery Fund
Point of Pride’s Annual Transgender Surgery Fund is a scholarship-like program that provides direct financial assistance to trans folks who cannot afford their gender-affirming surgery.
Learn More
HRT Access Fund
The HRT Access Fund provides 18 months of free medical care for trans folks seeking gender-affirming hormone therapy (also known as “HRT.”)
Learn More
Electrolysis Support Fund
The Electrolysis Support Fund provides financial assistance towards permanent hair removal services (including electrolysis and laser hair removal) for trans folks who cannot otherwise afford them.
Learn More
Thrive Fund
The Thrive Fund provides small grants to trans folks who cannot afford gender-affirming health and wellness needs including wigs, prosthetics, fertility preservation, vocal training, non-permanent forms of hair removal, medical tattooing, and more.
Learn More
Free Chest Binders & Femme Shapewear
Point of Pride provides free chest binders and free femme shapewear/gaffs —specially-designed, gender-affirming compression garments—to any trans person who needs one they cannot afford or safely obtain.
