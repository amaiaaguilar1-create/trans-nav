APPLICATIONS FOR HELP BY PHONE ARE AVAILABLE 9:00 AM TO 1:00PM
ON MONDAY, WEDNESDAY & THURSDAY ONLY.
All calls for assistance are directed to the Advocacy Referral Team. You may wait to speak to an available Intake specialist or leave a message to request a callback. You may leave a message between 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM on Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays. We attempt to return all calls within 3-5 business days to conduct an initial intake screening.
You also may apply for services online by clicking on the “Apply Now” tab above.
At the time of application, please be prepared to provide information about your household income and assets.
If you have received a summons to appear in court, please have this information with you so you can provide information about the specific court, opposing party and court date.
Legal Services cannot handle personal injury, traffic law, bankruptcy or criminal cases.
Legal Services also does not handle family law cases unless directly related to domestic violence or abuse.
Emergency Help
If you are in immediate danger, please dial 911.
If you need emergency shelter, call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 800.799.7233.
