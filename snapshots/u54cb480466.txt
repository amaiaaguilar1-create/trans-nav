MaineCare
What is MaineCare?
MaineCare is the federal Medicaid program in the state of Maine. The Office for Family Independence determines eligibility and ongoing enrollment for MaineCare.MaineCare provides free and low-cost health insurance to Mainers who meet certain requirements, based on household composition and income.
There are also additional options for people with disabilities and certain health conditions, young adults who have been in foster care, and those who need long-term care services (nursing facility, residential care, etc.).
What can MaineCare cover?
MaineCare can cover healthcare expenses such as:
doctor visits,
emergency medical care,
substance use treatment,
prescription drugs, and more.

How do I apply for MaineCare?
Option 1: Apply online through My Maine Connection .
Option 2: Download the MaineCare Application and mail it to:
Office for Family Independence
114 Corn Shop Lane
Farmington, ME 04938
Option 3: E-mail an application to Farmington.DHHS@Maine.gov
Option 4: Fax an application to (207) 778-8429 .
Option 5: Visit one of our district offices and apply in person.
After applying for MaineCare
After you submit an application, you will be contacted if any additional documents or information is needed (i.e. pay stubs, immigration documents, etc.). Once we can make an eligibility decision, you will receive a letter explaining if you are denied (with a reason why) or approved and enrolled in the MaineCare program.
Note: The Office for Family Independence (OFI) is responsible for determining eligibility for MaineCare applications within 45 days. Once enrolled by OFI, the Office of MaineCare Services (OMS) administers the program services and handles all billing and claims.
MaineCare Information for Members
People eligible for MaineCare are called MaineCare members. If you are a MaineCare member and have questions about what is covered for MaineCare services, need help finding a provider, or have other questions about using your healthcare, please visit the Office of MaineCare Services for assistance.
Paying Premiums Online
OFI has an Online Payment Portal for members to make online payments for MaineCare Premiums. It's an efficient and secure way for premium payments to be made online, instead of sending a payment by mail each month. This portal serves as another option to make these payments and is not mandatory; payments will still be accepted by mail. Please note that there is a $2 fee that will be assessed for each payment made on this Online Payment Portal. To make an online payment, visit: www.maine.gov/dhhs/paymentportal .
Tax Information
Form 1095-B is an informational health insurance tax form. It shows that you and/or your dependents had Minimum Essential Coverage (MEC) through Medicaid (MaineCare in Maine) for part or all of the year. The IRS no longer imposes tax penalties for not having health insurance and not submitting Form 1095-B with your tax return. MaineCare members may receive a copy of their 2025 IRS Form 1095-B upon request.
Starting February 17, 2026, to request a copy of your Form 1095-B, you can:
Call the Office for Family Independence at 1-855-797-4357;
Email your request to Farmington.DHHS@maine.gov ; or
Mail your request to: Office for Family Independence 114 Corn Shop Lane; Farmington, ME 04938
