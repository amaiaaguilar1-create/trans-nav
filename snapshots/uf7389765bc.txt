HIV prevention is different for everyone.
PrEP and PEP are HIV prevention medications that may be right for you.
What is PrEP?
PrEP, or pre-exposure prophylaxis, is medicine people at risk for HIV take to prevent getting HIV from sex or injection drug use. PrEP can stop HIV from taking hold and spreading throughout your body. Learn More
What is PEP?
HIV PEP, or post-exposure prophylaxis, is a 28-day course of daily oral HIV medicines taken within 72 hours after a possible exposure to HIV to prevent the virus from taking hold in your body. Learn More
Are PrEP and PEP covered by Insurance?
Under the Affordable Care Act, PrEP is free under almost all health insurance plans. Most insurances including Medicaid cover PEP medications. There are also patient assistance programs and co-pay assistance through the medication manufacturers.
