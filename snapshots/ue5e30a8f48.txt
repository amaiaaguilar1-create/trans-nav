State DMV fees
Fees are established by the legislature or promulgated in rule.
State driver license offices accept the following forms of payment:
Cash
Check (made payable to DOR)
Credit Card: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover Card
Contactless Pay: Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay
Payroll and 3rd party checks are not accepted
Short check fees must be paid by cash or money order only
Fees cannot be combined for credit card charges or payments made by check
An individual of any age whose driver license has been lost, stolen, or destroyed is eligible to receive a duplicate.
