Renew or Replace your Driver License or State ID Card.
If you already have an Oklahoma Driver License or State Identification Card, you can renew or replace it online or in person at a Licensed Operator, or Service Oklahoma Licensing Office.

Are You REAL ID Ready?
Beginning May 7, 2025, a REAL ID compliant Driver License or ID card, or a valid federal identification document (i.e. U.S. Passport) will be required for you to fly within the U.S. or visit a federal facility.

To surrender your Driver License for a State ID card in Oklahoma, visit a Licensed Operator or a Service Oklahoma Licensing Office. Bring your current driver's license as your primary form of identification.
At the office, inform the staff that you wish to surrender your license and obtain a state ID. You will need to:
Surrender your current Driver License
Sign a Voluntary Downgrade Form
Complete the process to obtain a State ID Card
A replacement costs $25.
