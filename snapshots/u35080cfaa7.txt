​​​Sexual Health
The Anchorage Public Health Clinic offers the following services&#58;
Please make an appointment, sometimes you can walk-in if there are open appointments. Our clinic can offer confidential sexual health services for individuals ages 13 and up. For teens, we do not need a parent/guardian or permissions to get confidential services.&#160; Our billing is sliding scale, and we can work with you on low-cost payment plans.
Testing and treatment for sexually transmitted infections (including sexual contacts)
HIV and Hepatitis C testing (results available same day)
PrEP Prescriptions (Pre-exposure prophylaxis to prevent HIV infection)​​
Pregnancy testing
Contraception & Family Planning​
Women’s Health annual exams​ including pelvic and pap smears
FREE condoms​ ​
