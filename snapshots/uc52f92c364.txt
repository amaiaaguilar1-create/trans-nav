Title 12. Civil Procedure
Chapter 33 - Change of Name
Section 1635 - Judgment - Authenticated Copy
Cite as: O.S. �, __ __
The judgment shall recite generally the material
facts and the change granted, or if denied, the reasons for the denial. A
certified or authenticated copy of such judgment may be filed in any office,
where proper to do so, and shall be regarded as a judgment in a civil
action.
