GENDER-AFFIRMING MEDICINE
EMPOWERING YOU IN YOUR GENDER JOURNEY
Your gender affirmation is individual to you. Whether you are looking for medical and surgical interventions or just want to see a medical provider who uses the right name and pronouns, we’re here for you.
Gender-affirming medicine, or GAM, means you are the best person to make decisions regarding your own body. We’re here to give you the tools and necessary information to make an informed decision.
Our GAM services include:
In-person and video visits
Hormone administration and monitoring for binary and nonbinary affirmations
Medicine regimens to meet your individual needs and goals (including microdosing hormones, using lower doses of medications, and working with different medication combinations)
In-office blood draws
Safe injection trainings
Pre-operative and post-operative exams for gender-affirming surgeries, along with guidance around specific surgical procedures
Navigation assistance for gender-affirming surgeries and procedures
Pelvic exams and Pap smears
Physical exams respectful of body autonomy
Referrals to LGBTQ-competent specialists
