University of Iowa Human Rights Policy
States that in no aspect of its programs shall there be differences in the treatment of persons because of race, creed, color, national origin, age, sex, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other classification that deprives the person of consideration as an individual.
You have a right to expert health care, delivered by providers who listen to your concerns, address your needs, and respect your confidentiality.
At the University of Iowa Health Care LGBTQ Clinic, you’ll find the advanced, comprehensive health care you need in the welcoming, inclusive environment you deserve.
Our specialized care teams follow national and international guidelines to provide supportive, state-of-the-art care specific to our lesbian, gay, bisexual, transgender, queer, and questioning patients — including patients with disabilities and non-English speakers.
Please note that current Iowa law prohibits certain gender-affirming care for patients under the age of 18. We provide services in compliance with applicable law.
Inclusive, multidisciplinary care — all in one place
From routine checkups and preventative care to mental health services and chronic disease management, the LGBTQ Clinic is a safe space for you to turn for all your health care needs.
Here, you’ll have access to a wide range of experts who are specially trained to provide compassionate, full-service care specific to your LGBTQ identity, including family medicine providers, urologists, obstetrician gynecologists, plastic and reconstructive surgeons, gastroenterologists, otolaryngologists, therapists, pharmacists, and more.
And, as part of the broader UI Health Care system, we can easily coordinate with any other specialists or providers involved in your care, providing support and advocacy when you need it.
