Welcome To
Nationz Foundation
We were incorporated on May 27, 2015, with the mission to support the Greater Richmond LGBTQIA+ community with HIV and STI testing, linkage to care, and access to holistic wellness services.
We are located at 1603 Santa Rosa Rd, Ste. 101, Henrico, VA 23229 on the bus line. Our hours of operation are Monday through Friday from 10:00 A.M. to 6:00 P.M. and every fourth Saturday from 10 A.M. to 1 P.M.

Our Mission Is Awareness
Nationz is one of the leading providers of HIV/AIDS prevention and advocacy in the Greater Richmond area. Building on years of experience and expertise, we understand the reality of HIV/AIDS and hope to promote a healthy existence for all.
