Payment Information
We can bill most insurance providers for your medical services and many prescription plans for your birth control, medication, and refills.
Please bring the following documents if you have them:
Birth Certificate
Pay Stub
Photo ID
Proof of Residence
You must pay on the day of your appointment unless you’ve made other arrangements.
Payment plans are available for many services. Our helpful staff can answer questions you may have about coverage.

Walk-in Information
You are welcome to walk in any time the Health Center is open to make an appointment or see if there is a same day appointment available. If you have the time to wait, we will make every effort to fit you in the same day. If we can’t see you, we will give you the first priority appointment available.

Appointment Policies and Information
Mask use within our health centers is not mandated. However, if you would like our staff to wear a mask during your visit, let us know. If you cannot attend your scheduled appointment, please let us know as soon as possible so we can make your appointment available to another person in need.
During inclement weather, please call 800-769-0045 to verify our health center's schedule.
Services Offered
You can fill your birth control prescriptions at our health center instead of the pharmacy. Available during regular business hours.
