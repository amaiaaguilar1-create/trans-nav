LGBTQ+ youth often lack access to inclusive care
Traditional healthcare and wellness programs may not be LGBTQ+ affirming, which leads to negative experiences and avoidance of seeking out wellness programs and services.
The mental health disparities between LGBTQ+ youth and their peers
LGBTQ+ youth and young adults are at a higher risk for depression, anxiety, and suicidal ideation. Our programs connect youth with health &#038; wellness services and create a support system for their overall well-being.
LGBTQ+ youth and young adults experience higher levels of stress than their peers
Discrimination, prejudice, and social isolation are just some of the factors that contribute to LGBTQ+ youth experiencing higher levels of stress. Our health &#038; wellness programs help equip them with coping mechanisms and build resilience.

DAASH
DAASH (Developmental & Age-Appropriate Sexual Health) is our monthly developmentally & age-appropriate, medically accurate, LGBTQ+ inclusive sexual health program designed specifically for LGBTQ+ youth & young adults. This inclusive program celebrates body diversity, gender identity, and sexual orientation. Topics include:
Healthy Relationships & Dating
Consent, Negotiation, & Refusal
Asexuality & Aromanticism
Trans & Non-Binary Relationships
STI & Safer Sex Basics
Sexting & Online Safety
Intimacy Beyond Sex & Sex Readiness
Trans & Gender Expansive support programs
Our Trans & Gender Expansive support programs provide a safe space to discuss gender-affirming health topics and empower transgender, non-binary, and gender expansive youth. Topics include:
Pronouns Support
Hormones 101
Gender Affirming Surgeries 101
Managing Gender Dysphoria
Safe Binding & Tucking Techniques
Finding LGBTQ+ Affirming Healthcare Providers
Supporting a Healthy Transition
