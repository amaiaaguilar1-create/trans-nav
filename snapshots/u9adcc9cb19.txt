Amending an Oklahoma Birth or Death Record
When is an amendment required?
An Oklahoma birth or death certificate may require an amendment if the information was:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>Recorded incorrectly by a healthcare provider or funeral director</li>\n<li>Provided inaccurately by a parent or informant</li>\n<li>Missing at the time of registration</li>\n<li>Legally changed later (such as a name change)</li>\n</ul>\n<p>By law, all amended records are marked “Amended.”&nbsp;<span style=\&#34;letter-spacing: 0.43px;\&#34;>The record will also show the amendment date and a brief description of the supporting evidence.</span></p>\n&#34;}}" id="text-e8fcf0a11f" class="cmp-text">
An Oklahoma birth or death certificate may require an amendment if the information was:
Recorded incorrectly by a healthcare provider or funeral director
Provided inaccurately by a parent or informant
Missing at the time of registration
Legally changed later (such as a name change)
By law, all amended records are marked “Amended.” The record will also show the amendment date and a brief description of the supporting evidence.
How Amendment Requests Are Reviewed
All amendment requests are reviewed to ensure:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>The applicant’s legal eligibility</li>\n<li>Any previous amendments to the record</li>\n<li>Any court or administrative actions that have already been taken</li>\n<li>Valid supporting documentation</li>\n<li>Instructions are given on a case-by-case basis depending on the type of correction requested.</li>\n</ul>\n<p>Check your child’s birth certificate as soon as it arrives. Ensure all details, like names and dates, are accurate. Correcting errors early helps avoid issues later.</p>\n&#34;}}" id="text-bccfdca674" class="cmp-text">
All amendment requests are reviewed to ensure:

Replacing or Sealing Oklahoma Birth Records
Birth records are <b>sealed and replaced</b>—without being marked “Amended”—only when <b>legal parentage changes</b>, such as:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>Adoption</li>\n<li>Paternity</li>\n</ul>\n<p>These records are confidential by law. The original record is sealed, and the final record does not show an amendment.</p>\n&#34;}}" id="text-dd7712defa" class="cmp-text">
Birth records are sealed and replaced —without being marked “Amended”—only when legal parentage changes , such as:
Adoption
Paternity
These records are confidential by law. The original record is sealed, and the final record does not show an amendment.

If not, an administrative order will be required.</li>\n<li>Once the application is received and verified, Vital Records will provide instructions and contact information for the Administrative Law Judge’s clerk to obtain the petition and instructions to obtain the administrative order.</li>\n<li>Once the Administrative Law Judge has issued the order, the clerk will send it to Vital Records, and the ordered changes will be completed and the record will be issued to the applicant.</li>\n</ul>\n&#34;}}" id="text-ff79654421" class="cmp-text">
Updated correction timeframe (effective May 7, 2026)
HB 3931 allows for the correction period past the current window of 1 year following the initial issuance of the death record.
For amendment requests of a death record initially issued within the last year
The Funeral Home or Funeral Director of record may submit written or electronic affidavits to amend the death record.
For amendment requests of a death record issued more than 1 year prior
Applicant will need to submit an application request including fees ($35) and include a note that identifies any item(s) that need to be corrected.
If a district court order to amend the record has been issued, please provide a copy of the certified court order. If not, an administrative order will be required.
Once the application is received and verified, Vital Records will provide instructions and contact information for the Administrative Law Judge’s clerk to obtain the petition and instructions to obtain the administrative order.
Once the Administrative Law Judge has issued the order, the clerk will send it to Vital Records, and the ordered changes will be completed and the record will be issued to the applicant.
