File a Complaint or Seek Help and Other Assistance
The Department of Financial Regulation assists consumers in understanding their options in the industries of banking, insurance and securities by providing information and education. The Department also investigates complaints filed by citizens against insurance, securities, and banking companies, as well as credit unions, mortgage brokers and consumer finance products. Department staff respond promptly and completely to questions and complaints and assist consumers in resolving those complaints when possible.
Banking
File a complaint about a financial institution, including banks and credit unions chartered in Vermont, along with lenders, mortgage brokers, sales finance companies, debt adjusters, and money servicers that are registered in Vermont.

Insurance
File a complaint about an insurance company or agent doing business in Vermont, including auto, homeowners, life, annuity, major medical, dental, pharmacy benefits, long-term care, Medicare supplement, or other types of coverage.

Securities
