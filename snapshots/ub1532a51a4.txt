Prior to 7/5/72 the rate was 6%.
From 7/6/72 to 8/23/75 the rate was 8%.
From 8/24/75 to 8/30/81 the rate was 9%.
From 8/31/81 to 9/5/91 the rate was 14%.
From 9/6/91 to present, variable rate from month to month and now quarter to quarter as provided in 45-103 .
On 9/6/91, the interest rate on delinquent cases remained at the rate of the last order or decree in the case. (Welch vs. Welch , 246 Neb. 435). (Prior to Welch vs. Welch it was erroneously directed that on 9/6/91 the interest rate on all delinquent cases changed to 6.68%.)
The Interest Rate Schedule is published in the Nebraska Advance Sheets, available electronically in the Court News section of JUSTICE, the Nebraska Supreme Court Web site, and from the State Court Administrator.
