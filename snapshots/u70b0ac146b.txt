What is HealthSource RI?
HealthSource RI was created because buying health coverage is complicated. It’s our job to cut through the confusion and connect Rhode Islanders with affordable, high quality coverage. We are the state’s official marketplace for health insurance. That means we’re your only source for getting tax credits to lower the cost of your health coverage. If you don’t get affordable health insurance through a job or a family member, you can count on us to connect you with coverage that meets your needs and your budget. We also offer flexible health insurance options for small businesses.
Since we opened our doors in 2013, we’ve been a trusted resource for Rhode Islanders to get covered and stay covered.

Transitioning from Medicaid?
If you’re no longer eligible for Medicaid, we can help you find affordable
coverage through a Qualified Health Plan (QHP).
What You Should Know

You may qualify for financial assistance to help pay for your plan- most of our customers do.

Losing Medicaid coverage is a Qualifying Life Event , so you have 60 days before or after to enroll in new coverage.

Over 100 Navigators are available for assistance across Rhode Island, and many are fluent in languages other than English.
