401.010 Adult may have name changed by District Court.
Any person at least eighteen (18) years of age may have his or her name changed by the
District Court of the county in which he or she resides. If he or she resides on a United
States Army post, military reservation, or fort, his or her name may be changed by the
District Court of any county adjacent thereto.
Effective: March 22, 2013
