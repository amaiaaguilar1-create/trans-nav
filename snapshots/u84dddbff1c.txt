Walk-in Information
You are welcome to walk in any time the Health Center is open to make an appointment or see if there is a same day appointment available. If you have the time to wait, we will make every effort to fit you in the same day. If we can’t see you, we will give you the first priority appointment available.
Please remember that all walk-ins are available on a first come, first serve basis, as the schedule allows.

Appointment Policies and Information
Please check-in 15 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment.
Our parking lot is located behind the building, at the corner of 7th St and Orange St, and can be entered from either street.
We welcome walk-in appointments on a first come, first served basis, as the schedule allows. You can also schedule your own appointment online.
Specially trained staff and medical equipment are available for people with disabilities.
Childcare Policies
Only infants and toddlers in a car seat or stroller are allowed in the exam room. Please make arrangements for childcare for any other children.
Services Offered
You can fill your birth control prescriptions at our health center instead of the pharmacy. Please call ahead for pill pick-up.
