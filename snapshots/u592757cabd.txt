Free Legal Help
NLSP provides free civil legal assistance to low-income DC residents and does not ever charge clients for legal services.
Who We Are:
Neighborhood Legal Services Program (NLSP) is a nonprofit legal services organization that provides free legal information, advice and representation to low-income District of Columbia residents on civil legal matters. We are committed to equal access to justice for all members of our community. NLSP is committed to working towards a more just and equitable Washington D.C. for all residents.
Please note that NLSP will be closed on October 12 and November 11, 2026.
