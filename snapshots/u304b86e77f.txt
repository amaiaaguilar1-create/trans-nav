Payment by Check: The Probate Court does not accept personal checks. Money orders, cashier's check, and attorney business checks should be made payable to: Cuyahoga County Probate Court.
Note: Payments to multiple cases require a separate check; payments for multiple cases 'bundled' onto a single check will be returned.
All online requests for copies require payment by credit or debit card. Payments are processed by Point-N-Pay, which charges a transaction service fee. Visit the Copy Requests page to learn more.
The cost information covers basic costs only. Additional filings have additional costs. For additional cost information, please contact the appropriate department:
Clerk's Office (for costs on existing cases): (216) 443-8785 OR (216) 443-8895
Marriage Department (for original or copies of marriage certificates): (216) 443-8920
Quality Assurance Department (for all other copies or certified copies): (216) 443-8792
