FAQ: STD and STI Testing and Treatment
When Should I Get Tested After Exposure?
This varies depending on the specific infection. HIV testing has a detection window of 18-45 days, depending on the type of test used. Chlamydia and gonorrhea can typically be detected through testing within 1-2 weeks. In some cases, it could take up to 3 weeks for syphilis to become detectable.
Contact our STD Clinic for a consultation. We'll provide the appropriate testing at the right time for each possible infection, and follow up with additional testing as needed.
Do I Need an Appointment to Be Tested?
We offer both on-site and telehealth appointments. While walk-ins are welcome during normal operating hours, pre-scheduling an appointment can save you time.
Are STD Tests Confidential?
Under South Carolina law, your test results for sexually transmitted diseases are confidential. We don't disclose your test results to employers or family members. While the state department of health may receive notifications of reportable infections, like HIV or syphilis, your identity is protected in accordance with applicable privacy laws and regulations.

HIV

If you're not sure what to expect based on your medical history and any symptoms you've experienced, our helpful team can assist.
If I Test Positive, What Happens Next?
Your results will be reviewed by our experienced team, who will discuss STI treatment options with you. Most sexually transmitted diseases, such as chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, and trichomoniasis, are treatable using antibiotics. HIV and herpes can be managed using ongoing medications.
All STD treatment options are provided on site, so you will not need to seek additional STD care from another source.
Can I Receive Treatment From the Same STI Clinic Where I Was Tested?
Yes, we provide both STD testing and STD treatment on-site. If you test positive for a bacterial disease, like chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, or trichomoniasis, you may be able to receive treatment the same day.
What Will STD Testing Cost Me?
Our costs vary based on the number and types of tests that we order. We participate in the Medicare program, Medicaid program, and most major commercial and private insurance plans. Sliding-scale fees and grant funding may be available to individuals without insurance coverage or whose income is below certain thresholds.
