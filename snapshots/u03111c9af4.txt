Remote Expungement/Pardon Clinics&#8203;
The Expungement/Pardon clinic, staffed by volunteer attorneys and law students, provides free, confidential, and brief legal advice and referrals for Wisconsin expungement and pardon applications.
This clinic is for criminal law issues, not eviction cases. For eviction related questions, see our Eviction Diversion Initiative .
