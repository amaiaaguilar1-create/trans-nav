Filing a Complaint
We encourage consumers to speak with an Insurance Division investigator before filing a complaint. In some cases, a resolution is possible without filing a formal complaint with the division. Please call 1-844-808-DCCA (3222) between 7:45AM &#8211; 4:30PM (Hawaiʻi Standard Time), Monday to Friday, except state holidays.
Online Complaint Form
If you have a health insurance complaint , please see below for circumstances that require specific actions.
Against insurers licensed in other jurisdictions should be addressed to the insurance commissioner of the state in which the health plan was issued
Medicare – contact CMS (Center for Medicare and Medicaid Services) at 1-800-633-4227; or contact Sage Plus at (888) 875-9229
Medicaid (QUEST) – Plan members should address their concerns to the Med-QUEST Customer Service Section at 524-3370 (Oahu) and (800) 316-8005 (neighbor island) and health care providers should present their concerns to the Med-QUEST Provider Hotline at 692-8099.
Military Health Plans – contact CHAMPVA at (800) 733 8387, or Tricare at 1-800-242-6788
Employer Self-Funded Plans – contact your employer for assistance direction, or the Employee Benefit Security Administration at 1-866-444-3272 if you need additional help
Federal Employee Health Benefit Plans – contact your employer, or consider filing a complaint with the United States Office of Personnel Management at the following address: United States Office of Personnel Management, Insurance Services Programs, Health Insurance Group 2, 1900 E Street, NW, Washington DC 20415
