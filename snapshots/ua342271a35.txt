We won’t pretend to have all the answers, and we won’t tell you everything will be fine. What we will do is listen without judgment, stay with you in the moment, and help you find a way forward — at your pace, on your terms.
You are not a burden. We want to hear from you.

You are actively in a crisis.

You are grieving.
You are struggling with suicidal ideation.

You are searching for local resources but not sure where to start.
You want to test the line out to become more comfortable with using it.
You want to demonstrate how the line works to your child, loved one, or client.
