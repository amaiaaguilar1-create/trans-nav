This initiative is a vital step in providing support within the community, especially as it seeks to address the need for lgbt homeless shelters for adults in NYC and enhance access to lgbtq shelter options across the city.

Destination Tomorrow
Destination Tomorrow is a grassroots agency and the LGBTQ+ center of the Bronx borough. We firmly believe that no individual should have to leave their neighborhood to access vital LGBTQ+ specific services. It is our conviction that all LGBTQ+ young people, including those in the gay Bronx community, deserve a safe space to grow, learn, and flourish. Furthermore, we believe that people, regardless of their sexual identity or gender expression, should have a space they can truly call their own.
Through our extensive programming and services—both in-house and through referrals—we diligently work to ensure that Bronx LGBTQ+ community members, along with their supporters, have access to this essential space. Additionally, we offer capacity building for agencies aiming to support LGBTQ+ communities who require the cultural competency and expertise to do so effectively.
Mission:
Our mission centers on empowering the most vulnerable LGBTQ+ community members, aiming to redirect them from the path of needing emergency housing solutions, such as LGBT homeless shelters for adults in NYC. The organization focuses on economic, social, and mental empowerment through a wide range of holistic educational, financial, support-based, housing, and health programs.
Vision:
As we provide consistent, necessary, and empowering resources to our LGBTQ+ clients—including those seeking LGBTQ shelter in NYC—we envision a future that prioritizes prevention, avoiding crisis situations through proactive action plans and community connections.
Bronx walk-in hours are Monday to Friday from 10 am to 6 pm, with the last client taken at 5 pm. We are located at 452 E 149th St, 3rd floor, Bronx, New York 10455.
