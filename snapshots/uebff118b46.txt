The TWF offers direct financial assistance to transgender, nonbinary and gender expansive community members. These funds are specifically intended to assist community members with barriers to gender-affirming medical care. For example:
Appointments with healthcare providers needed for gender-affirming care
Prescriptions or medical supplies
Logistical support for accessing an affirming provider, including but not limited to, travel, childcare, and lodging.
Financial coverage for medical procedures
