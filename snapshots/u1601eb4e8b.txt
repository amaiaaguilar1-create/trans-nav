Frequently Asked Questions
The Commonwealth Healthcare Corporation (CHCC) FAQ page provides quick and reliable answers to commonly asked
questions about our healthcare services, programs, and patient support. Whether you're seeking information on
appointments, insurance, or community health initiatives, our FAQ page is here to guide you.
What are the work hours for the Commonwealth Healthcare Corporation?
CHCC operates 24/7 for emergency services, while other departments, such as outpatient clinics,
typically operate Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM. For specific department hours, please
visit our Medical Services page. For more information, you can contact us at
670-234-8950
Where can I see upcoming events, outreaches, and hospital activities?
Stay informed about health programs, clinics, and community events by checking our CHCC Calendar
How can I request a copy of my medical records?
To request your medical records, you need to submit a written request to the Medical Records Department.
You can download the "Authorization to Release Medical Information" form from the CHCC website, complete
it, and ensure it's signed by you or your legal representative. Duplication fees may apply and must be
paid in advance. For assistance, contact the Medical Records office at (670) 236-8356 during regular
business hours.
What services does the Immunization Program offer, and how can I contact them?
The Immunization Program aims to prevent vaccine-preventable diseases by increasing and maintaining high
immunization coverage. Services include vaccinations, temporary health certificates, community outreach
and education, and enforcement of public health laws. The program operates
Monday to Friday from 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM at the CHCC Main Campus - Public Health. For more information,
call (670) 236-8745 or email immunization@chcc.health. To learn more, please visit Immunization - CHCC | Public Health Services
What are the hours and services of the CHCC Outpatient Pharmacy?
The CHCC Outpatient Pharmacy is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, and on Saturdays,
Sundays, and holidays from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM. Located at the CHCC Main Campus, the pharmacy dispenses
prescription drugs for outpatient use and offers over-the-counter medications and health products. For
inquiries, call (670) 236-8798.
