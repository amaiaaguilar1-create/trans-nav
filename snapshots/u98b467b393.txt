Free Name Change Clinic in Cookeville, Tennessee – July 9, 2026
Get Free Legal Help with Your Name Change in Cookeville, TN
Do you need to legally change your name in Tennessee? Legal Aid Society of Middle Tennessee and the Cumberlands is hosting a free name change clinic in Cookeville, TN on Thursday, July 9, 2026.
What Is a Legal Name Change?
A legal name change is a court process that officially updates your name on government records, identification, and legal documents. Having the correct legal name is essential for accessing employment, housing, healthcare, and other vital services. Our attorneys can help guide you through the process at no cost.
