Transgender females
Estrogen Therapy
Oral estradiol 2.0-6.0 mg/day
Transdermal estradiol path (new patch placed every 0.025-0.2 mg/day
3-5 days)
Parenteral estradiol valerate or cypionate ● 5-30 mg IM every 2 weeks
● 2-10 mg IM every week
Anti-androgens
Spironolactone 100-300 mg/day
Transgender males
Testosterone Therapy
Parenteral testosterone enanthate or cypionate 100–200 mg sequentially IM every 2 weeks or SC 50% per week
Parenteral undecanoate 1,000 mg initially, followed by an injection at 6 weeks then at 12-week
(non-preferred product) intervals
Transdermal testosterone gel 1.6% 50-100 mg/day
Transdermal testosterone patch 2.5-7.5 mg/day
Abbreviations: IM, intramuscularly; SC, subcutaneously.
