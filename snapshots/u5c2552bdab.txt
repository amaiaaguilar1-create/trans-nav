Where can I get medical help?
For a directory of over 200 healthcare providers that have proactively identified themselves as LGBTQ-friendly, view Garden State Equality's Affirming Healthcare Map .
Three specific centers are highlighted below:
Rutgers Center for Transgender Health : The Rutgers Center for Transgender Health is a multidisciplinary center that provides a full spectrum of medical and surgical care for transgender and non-binary individuals.
Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital - PROUD Center : The Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital provides comprehensive services and care for transgender individuals including primary care services, hormone therapy, support groups, and mental health support.
VNACJ's Community Health Center's LGBTQ Center for Health & Wellness provides medical services for the transgender and broader LGBTQIA+ community including endocrinology / hormone replacement therapy and support groups. Appointments must be made by calling 732-502-5130.
NOTE: Providers who are affiliated with RWJUH and Rutgers School of Medicine have passed these institution's strenuous quality standards and can be counted upon to deliver high quality services.
Source: https://www.nj.gov/dcf/adolescent/lgbtqi/
