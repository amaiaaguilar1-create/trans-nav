Driver License/ID Fees and Exemptions
Most driver licenses are valid for eight years. Licenses
issued to those 65 or older and Driver Authorization Cards are valid for
four years.
Driver Authorization Cards are charged the same fees as
non-commercial Driver Licenses. Commercial license fees are separate.
If a license or ID card is issued for less than four
years, the four-year fee still applies.

Fee Exemptions
Homeless - Age 25 and Older
The DMV may waive the fee for a duplicate ID card for individuals 25 years or older who declare themselves homeless.
Must be a one-time exemption.
Applicants must apply in person.
Declaration of Homeless Status (DMV 128)
Homeless - Under 25
Individuals under 25 who declare themselves homeless are eligible for full fee exemptions for:
Original
Renewal
Duplicate Nevada Driver’s License, ID Card, or Driver Authorization Card
No reimbursement is required.
Declaration of Homeless Status (DMV 128)
Released Offenders
The DMV may waive the $21.50 duplicate fee for individuals released from prison or jail within the past 90 days.
Must provide official documentation of release from:
Nevada Department of Corrections
Equivalent agency in another state
Local jail
This is a one-time exemption for duplicates only. Must apply in person and complete DMV190 form
Request for Waiver of Duplicate Fees- Released Prisoners (DP 190)
Photo Fee May Apply at Renewal:
You may be required to reimburse the $3.50 photo fee to the DMV at the time of your next regular renewal if you are employed at the time of renewal.

New residents obtaining a Nevada license or ID for the first time.
