Genderbands' Future
A message from the Genderbands Board:
After deep reflection and careful planning, the Genderbands Board of Directors is sharing an important update about the future of our work.
Genderbands began as a deeply personal act of care. Founder Ian Giles originally created Genderbands as a way to fundraise for their own top surgery. When that goal was met, Ian made a deliberate choice: instead of closing that chapter, they wanted to offer the same sense of possibility and peace of mind to others. What began as individual fundraising grew into a community-supported grant program, sustained by donations from allies and community members who believed in mutual aid and access to gender-affirming care.
As we look ahead, the Board is working to transfer Genderbands' programs, assets, and remaining finances to local organizations whose values, leadership, and community accountability closely align with our own. This decision was not made lightly and followed months of discussion focused on the long-term best interest of the trans community.
We know change can bring questions and uncertainty. Genderbands' legacy does not end here. It continues through the values that shaped it and the trans-led work that will live on.
We are deeply grateful to everyone who has supported Genderbands over the years: donors, volunteers, partners, artists, organizers, and community members. This transition is rooted in that collective effort and in our belief that our mission is bigger than any single organization.
With care and gratitude,
The Genderbands
