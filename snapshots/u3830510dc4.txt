Medical Care
Primary Care
Our primary care providers help you manage your overall health through annual physicals, preventive screenings, immunizations, routine checkups, and personalized treatment plans.
We focus on early detection, disease prevention, and helping you achieve your long-term health goals.
Pediatrics
We provide compassionate healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents, including well-child visits, immunizations, developmental screenings, hearing and vision services, and routine preventive care. Our team works closely with families to support healthy growth and development at every age.
Women&#8217;s Health
Our women&#8217;s health services focus on preventive care and wellness, including annual exams, Pap smears, breast health screenings, health education, and follow-up care. We also coordinate referrals for specialized gynecological and obstetrical services when needed.
Chronic Disease Management
Managing a chronic condition requires ongoing support and coordinated care. Our healthcare team works with patients living with diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, HIV, and other long-term health conditions through treatment planning, routine monitoring, patient education, and personalized care designed to improve long-term health outcomes.
Remote Patient Monitoring
Our Remote Patient Monitoring program helps eligible patients stay connected to their healthcare team between appointments. Using secure monitoring technology and connected devices, patients can track important health information from home while receiving ongoing support for chronic conditions and treatment goals.
Health Promotion & Prevention
Prevention is a key part of staying healthy. Our health promotion services provide education, screenings, and prevention strategies that help patients take control of their health and reduce their risk of disease. We offer confidential testing, prevention resources, and clinical support in a welcoming, judgment-free environment.
HIV & STI Testing – Get confidential HIV and STI testing with expert support, prevention guidance, and connections to care when needed.
PrEP &#8211; PrEP (Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis) is a highly effective HIV prevention option. We offer same-day oral PrEP, long-acting injectable PrEP, and personalized support to help you choose the best option.
PEP &#8211; PEP is an emergency HIV prevention treatment that must be started within 72 hours of possible exposure. Our team can help you quickly access care and next steps.
DoxyPEP &#8211; DoxyPEP is a prevention strategy that may help reduce the risk of certain sexually transmitted infections after potential exposure. Our providers can help determine if it&#8217;s right for you.
