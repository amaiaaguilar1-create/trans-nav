Transgender Healthcare Services at Whitman‑Walker
We strive to provide a safer, respectful, and affirming environment that meets the needs and supports the livelihood of transgender people.
From medical, behavioral health and all of our support services, you deserve care that treats you as an individual and honors your experiences. Do you have questions about starting hormone therapy? Looking to learn more about surgery support, explore your options for mental health care, or want help with a legal name and gender marker change on documents?
Finding quality, safer, and stigma-free health care can be challenging. Whitman-Walker Health team members are here to support you.
Whitman-Walker Health provides hormone therapy to patients after engagement in healthcare services and with necessary consents to treat.
