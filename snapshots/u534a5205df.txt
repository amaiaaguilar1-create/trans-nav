The pro bono and civic education programs team of the North Carolina Bar Foundation supports NCBA Sections, Divisions and Committees to organize pro bono and civic education initiatives that engage their members in service. If you are an NCBA member with an interest in organizing a pro bono or civic education initiative with your NCBA Section, Division or Committee, please reach out to us at [email&#160;protected] to learn more about ways that NCBA+NCBF staff and resources are available to support your initiative.
LGBTQ+ Legal Clinics
Hosted by the NCBA Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity Committee, clinics support transgender North Carolinians seeking a legal name change.
Pro Se Custody Clinics
Hosted by the NCBA Paralegal Division and Family Law Section, this project assists low-income parents with completing self-serve custody packets.
Equity Preservation Project
Hosted by the NCBA Elder and Special Needs Law Section, this project seeks to break the cycle of poverty reinforced by Medicaid estate recovery policy.
Student-Athlete Pro Bono Project
Hosted by the NCBA Sports and Entertainment Law Section, this project helps prevent student-athletes from being taken advantage of.
Veterans Life Center Pro Bono Clinic
Hosted by the NCBA Military & Veterans Law Section, this project provides pro bono limited legal services, clinics and pro se assistance to homeless veterans.
Naturalization + Refugee Assistance Project
Hosted by the NCBA Paralegal Division and International Practice Section, this project assists those pursuing Afghan Humanitarian Parolees status.
Opt-Inspire Pro Bono Project
Hosted by the NCBA Privacy and Data Security Section, this project educates senior citizens on how to navigate the digital landscape.
Tax Court Calendar Call Program
Hosted by the NCBA Tax Section, this program provides support for taxpayers with cases pending in U.S. Tax Court.
“Cornerstone Counsel” Serving Non-Profit Organizations
Hosted by the NCBA Construction Law Section, this project helps organizations navigate the complexities of capital improvement projects.
Heir Property Pro Bono Project
Hosted by the NCBA Real Property Section and other partners, this project assists legal services clients with heir property issues.
