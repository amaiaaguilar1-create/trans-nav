Family & Internal Medicine
Our interdisciplinary team of health care professionals provide accessible, comprehensive, quality services. We believe education, advocacy and connection are all part of whole person care, and we are committed to meeting the health care needs of you and your family every step of the way.
Services
Our family and internal medicine clinicians provide screening, diagnosis and treatment to help monitor you and your family’s health through services including but not limited to:
Case management and care coordination
Chronic disease care for diseases such as asthma, diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol and depression
Follow-up care
Physicals – Annual, school, sports and immigration
Testing and treatment for latent tuberculosis, STIs and HIV
