Housing individuals and families impacted by HIV since 1985.
Our Mission
Chicago House empowers people affected by HIV to lead healthy and dignified lives through housing and compassionate, client-centered support services.
We are committed to preventing new HIV transmissions and making sure individuals faced with a diagnosis are linked to care.

Our Impact
In 2025, we:
housed 400+ clients,
96% of which were medically adherent,
and had 2,500+ clients access our testing services.
500+ clients utilized our employment services
many coming from our TransSafe Drop-Ins.
