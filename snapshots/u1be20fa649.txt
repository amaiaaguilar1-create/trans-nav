Walk-in Information
You are welcome to walk in any time the Health Center is open to make an appointment or see if there is a same day appointment available. If you have the time to wait, we will make every effort to fit you in the same day. If we can’t see you, we will give you the first priority appointment available.
We will do our best to accommodate walk-ins for the following appointment types during our regular appointment schedule:
STI Screening
Problem Visits
Birth Control Visits
Depo Injections
Pregnancy Tests
Primary Care (follow-up visits)
Gender Affirming Care (follow-up visits)
Lab Only Visits
Walk-in availability is based on clinic capacity and provider schedules. For timely service, scheduled appointments are recommended. In addition to regular services, we also offer virtual health center options.
Payment is expected at the time of service for all visit types.
