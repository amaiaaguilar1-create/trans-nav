No one should have to earn care.
HIPS works alongside people shut out by traditional support systems. We bring healthcare, supplies and support to communities across D.C., without judgment.
Get to know HIPS
What showing up looks like.
On the street, HIPS teams distribute naloxone
and supplies that help people stay safer.
At our clinic and drop-in center, people find
healthcare, practical support and help
navigating what comes next.
Some needs are immediate. Others take time,
trust and more than one conversation.
HIPS is here for both.
Different settings. Different needs.
The same commitment: listen to people,
believe them and get to work.
