Transfusion
Transfusion is a peer-led, non-clinical support group committed to supporting the transgender and gender non-conforming & nonbinary community. Transfusion is meant to be a safe and supportive space to find community, share experiences, provide emotional support, and engage in facilitated conversation around a variety of topics. Meetings are on the third Wednesday of the month from 6 - 8 PM. There is no registration required, just drop in!
Visit our community calendar, check out our social media, or sign up for our emails to get details on this month’s meeting and for other updates!
