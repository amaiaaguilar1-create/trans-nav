Meet myDrive
Kentucky’s New Home for Online Driver Services
The Kentucky Transportation Cabinet (KYTC) is upgrading systems and launching myDrive , a new portal for managing your driver license and ID services online.
June 8-18
Office walk-in services will be available from 8 a.m. to 12 p.m. at all Driver Licensing Regional office locations.
Account Verification
To access online services, you’ll need to create an account and link it to your Kentucky credential. Customers will need a unique Letter ID alphanumeric code from a KYTC document dated on or after June 6, 2026, issued within the last 60 days to connect your account to your credential.
Don’t have one? Request a Letter ID by mail when creating your myDrive account or visit a Driver Licensing Regional Office. Delivery typically takes 5–10 business days .

Identification Cards
Kentucky offers several identification card options for customers who need a state-issued credential. Review information about ID card types, required documents, and renewal or update options.
ID cards can be obtained at any Driver Licensing Regional Office.
Required documents include proof of identity, Social Security, and residency.
Eligibility and fees may vary depending on the type of ID card requested.
Types of ID Cards
Review the ID card options below, including eligibility, fees, and required documents. ID cards must be applied for in person at a Driver Licensing Regional Office. Completing a pre-application through myDrive can help ensure you bring the correct documents and save time during your visit.
Standard ID Card
Applicants age 15 or older may apply at any Regional Office.
Bring a birth certificate, Social Security card, and proof of residency. View valid document types PDF .
If under 18, a parent or guardian may provide proof of residency showing the applicant lives with them.
A parent or guardian must be present for applicants under 18.

Kentucky Child ID Program
The Child ID Program supports the Kentucky State Police Missing Persons Clearinghouse and the Amber Alert System in cases involving missing children. A Child ID card may be obtained at any Regional Office.
Available for children ages 2–15.
Bring a birth certificate, Social Security card, and proof of residency. View valid document types PDF .
Child must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident.
A parent or guardian must be present to sign the application and verify citizenship.
Foster children must bring a Commitment Verification letter signed by their Independent Living Specialist. Custody papers are not required.
Child ID Card Format
Child ID cards are issued in a vertical format, and the child’s address does not appear on the face of the card.
This helps protect the child if the card is lost or stolen.

Important Eligibility Rules
Effective July 15, 2024, an applicant may not be issued a personal identification card if the applicant currently holds a valid instruction permit or operator’s license.
A person may not hold more than one license or personal identification card at the same time.
If a person’s instruction permit or operator’s license has been suspended or revoked, the person may be issued a personal identification card.
A personal identification card must be surrendered when the person applies to have his or her instruction permit or operator’s license reinstated.
Renewal, Replacement and Update Options
Eligible customers may renew their ID card online, by mail, or in person. Additional services—such as ordering a replacement, updating an address, completing a name change, or upgrading to a REAL ID—may also be available depending on eligibility.
Renew an ID Card
ID cards may be renewed online, by mail, or in person, depending on eligibility. Review the options below to determine which method is right for you.
Online (myDrive): Eligible customers may renew their Kentucky credential online through a verified myDrive account. Address changes can be completed as part of the renewal process. Please note that REAL ID upgrades are not available online and must be completed in person. Customers may select a 4-year or 8-year term lifespan.
By Mail: Eligible customers may renew by mail using the appropriate form. Address updates can be completed during the renewal. REAL ID upgrades are not available by mail.
In Person: Visit a Regional Office to renew your ID card, upgrade to a REAL ID, or complete a name change. All services are available in person.

Replace an ID Card
If your ID card has been lost, stolen, damaged, or destroyed, you may request a replacement.
Online (myDrive): Eligible customers may request a replacement online through the myDrive portal with a verified account. Address updates can be completed during the process. REAL ID upgrades are not available online.
By Mail: Eligible customers may request a replacement by mail using the appropriate form. Address updates can be completed during the process. REAL ID upgrades are not available by mail.
In Person: Visit a Regional Office to request a replacement, upgrade to a REAL ID, or complete a name change. All services are available in person.
A $15.00 replacement fee applies.
If you need to update your name, address, or upgrade to a REAL ID, additional documents may be required.

What documents are required for an ID card?
To apply for a Kentucky ID card, you will need:
Birth certificate
Social Security card
Proof of residency
Name change document, if your current full legal name, date of birth, or gender is different than what is displayed on your identity or lawful status document.
List of approved documents PDF
Can I have both a Kentucky driver's license or permit and an ID card?
No. Effective July 15, 2024, a person may not be issued a personal identification card if they currently hold a valid Kentucky instruction permit or operator’s license. A person may not hold more than one license or personal identification card at the same time.
Can I renew my ID card online?
Yes, if eligible. Customers may renew an ID card online through myDrive. A verified myDrive account is required.
myDrive Portal
Can I upgrade to a REAL ID online or by mail?
No. REAL ID upgrades cannot be completed online or by mail. You must visit a Regional Office and bring the required documents .
Can someone under 18 get an ID card?
Yes. If the applicant is under 18, a parent or guardian must be present. A parent or guardian may also provide proof of residency in their own name to show the applicant lives with them.
How can someone without a permanent address apply for an ID card?
If the applicant does not have a fixed or permanent address, they may provide a signed letter from a homeless shelter, health care facility, or social service agency stating they are currently receiving services and are a Kentucky resident. This letter may be used as proof of residency for a Homeless ID card.
What is the cost of an ID card?
A standard identification card costs $11.50 for four years or $23.00 for eight years. A Child ID card costs $6.00 for four years. Homeless ID cards and voter ID cards may be issued at no cost for customers who meet eligibility requirements.
What documents do I need to renew or replace my ID card?
If the information listed on your current ID card is up to date, you do not need to bring any documents to renew or request a replacement.
Additional documents are only required if you are:
Updating your name
Changing your address
Upgrading to a REAL ID
If you are renewing or replacing in person, bring any documents that apply to your specific situation to help avoid delays.
