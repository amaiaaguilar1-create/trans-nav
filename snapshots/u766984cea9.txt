Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual and Transgender Health Care
Denver Health LGBTQ+ Health Services
Denver Health’s LGBTQ+ Health Services is committed to a decentralized model of health care for LGBTQ+ people across Colorado – meaning that any LGBTQ+ person should be met with open, excellent, affirming care. Within the decentralized model, LGBTQ+ Health Services provides LGBTQ+ patients with patient navigation services for primary care needs and gender affirming surgery.
At Denver Health, our LGBTQ+ friendly doctors and staff are dedicated to providing high quality and welcoming health care for all, which means our team of medical providers, including physicians and nurses, is here to care for our LGBTQ+ patients through family and internal medicine, and mental health services.
Our team members communicate with established patients via MyChart, our online patient portal. If you are an established patient but don’t have an account, please register following this link: MyChart - signup page .
Our LGBTQ+ Health Services Include:
Preventive health screenings
Management of chronic conditions
Sexual health services
Same-day pre-exposure prophylaxis
HIV services
Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) on an Informed Consent Model
Voice therapy
Behavioral Health services
New Patient Paperwork
Gender Affirming Surgical Interest Form Primary Care New Patient Form
Please email a copy of your identification and insurance card to our secure inbox at LGBTadmin@dhha.org .
Patient Navigators: Dedicated to Assisting LGBTQ+ Patients
Our patient navigators are dedicated to ensuring that our lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgender patients are supported through their health care journey. All LGBTQ+ patients will be connected with welcoming and affirming medical providers for all of their health care needs.
If you would like additional support, patient navigators are here to attend medical appointments with you and will continue to be your advocates as you become familiar with our health care system. Please contact one of our dedicated LGBTQ+ patient navigators at 303-602-5699.
To learn more about LGBTQ+ Health Services, email LGBTadmin@dhha.org .
Accepted Insurances and Payment
Denver Health accepts Medicaid, Medicare and most commercial insurance plans. Please check with your insurance carrier to confirm Denver Health is in-network. To learn more about LGBTQ+ Health Services, please email LGBTadmin@dhha.org
