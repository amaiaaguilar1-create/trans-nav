TITLE: Gender Designation Changes on Driver Licenses and Identification
Cards
________________________________________________________________________
PURPOSE: This procedure establishes new standards for changing a gender
designation on a driver license or identification card by providing the
means for a person to change his/her gender designation without that
person having undergone gender reassignment surgery. These procedures
are in alignment with the U.S. Department of State’s requirements for
changing a gender designation on a U.S. passport as well as the majority
of other U.S. jurisdiction driver license and identification card issuance
agencies.
________________________________________________________________________
1.0 Customer Responsibilities
1.1 The customer must obtain Form MV2020 - Request for Gender Change on
Driver License/Identification Card. Form MV2020 can be obtained at any
DMV location or on the DMV website under “Forms and Manuals”.
1.1.1 The customer is not required to have completed gender
reassignment surgery. Instead, Section C must be completed by a
medical or social service provider licensed in the United States
certifying the customer’s gender is either male or female.
1.1.2 Once the form is completed, the customer must visit any DMV
location with the completed application.
1.1.3 Once a DMV representative verifies the information on Form
MV2020 the gender designation on the customer’s driver license
or identification card may be processed.
NOTE: Form MV2020 will not be processed via mail or on
line.
1.2 If additional changes need to be made to the customer’s record such as a
name change, the customer must present all necessary and typical
documentation needed for the change.
