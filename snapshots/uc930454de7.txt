Fee Waivers
A Motion to Waive Filing Fee and Service of Process Fee is a request to not be charged for filing a case or having legal papers delivered. Learn about the process and forms to submit.
You can make a request to the Court asking not to be charged for filing your case or for having the sheriff deliver legal papers. This request is called Motion, Affidavit, and Order to Waive Filing Fee and Service of Process Fee, which gives the Judge information about your finances. The Judge will use this information to decide if you qualify to have your fees waived.
You will need to submit the following document:
UJS-022 Motion, Affidavit, and Order to Waive Filing Fee & Service of Process Fee Form
If you do not hear from the clerk of court in five business days, you should contact the office and ask whether the Judge signed your Order on Motion to Waive Filing Fee & Service of Process Fee. If the Judge did not sign your Order, you have 30 days to pay the filing fee or your case will be dismissed.

Serving Someone
Service or "serving" someone is communicating with the other party to issue papers and informing them about the lawsuit. Service can be done in person, by mail, or by a sheriff&#x27;s office or private process server.
