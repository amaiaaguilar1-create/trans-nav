prism center
The Prism Center is South Dakota’s only LGBTQ2S+ community center.
The Prism Center serves as a functional space that combines the Transformation Project’s services into one cohesive location.
Door info: The Prism Center address is 500 N. Western Ave. Our doors are to the right of the recycling bins from the parking lot. You will have to ring the doorbell to be let in for safety purposes.

Do you live in rural South Dakota and aren’t able to make it to the Prism Center in person?
You can now join us virtually with the Prism Center Discord Server!!
The Prism Center Discord server is a unique virtual extension of our in-person center, designed to bridge the gap for LGBTQ2S+ individuals across South Dakota, especially those in rural areas. Recognizing that transportation can often be a barrier, our server ensures that everyone can participate in our programs, regardless of location. The server operates during our regular Hang Out Hours and for special programs. Currently open to all queer adults in the state. Join us to connect, learn, and grow in a supportive and accessible environment.
