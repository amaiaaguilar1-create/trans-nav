Credit Card Transaction Fee Information
Customers who choose to pay with a credit or debit card for transactions conducted through myBMV, at a branch, over the phone, and through BMV Connect kiosk, will incur a transaction fee of $0.40 + 2.06%. Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express are accepted.
Payment types accepted by medium:
myBMV: credit cards, debit cards, or e-check
Branches: credit cards, debit cards, cash, or check
BMV Connect Kiosks: credit cards, debit cards, or cash
Over the Phone: credit cards or debit cards
Mail: credit cards, debit cards, check, or e-check
Note: Checks should be made out to &ldquo;BMV&rdquo; or &ldquo;Bureau of Motor Vehicles&rdquo;.
