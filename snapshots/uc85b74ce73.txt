We provide HIV prevention education and case management services to the entire state of Nebraska and 11 counties in Southwest Iowa.
Founded in 1984 as a compassionate response to the AIDS crisis, Nebraska AIDS Project (NAP) leads the community in the fight to overcome HIV/AIDS and its stigma through education, supportive services, and advocacy. We are one of the few statewide AIDS service organizations in the country and the only community-based AIDS service organization in Nebraska.
