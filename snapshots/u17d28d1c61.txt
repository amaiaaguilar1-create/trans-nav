In compliance with N.J.S.A. 26:8-40.12, the State Registrar shall issue an amended certificate of birth to a person born in the State of New
Jersey who requests an amended certificate of birth which shows the gender and, if applicable, the new name of the person if it has been
changed. Specifically, “The State Registrar shall issue the amended certificate of birth upon receipt of: (1) a certified copy of an
order from a court of competent jurisdiction which indicates that the name of the person has been changed, if the person has
changed his or her name; and (2) a form provided by the State Registrar and completed by the person, […]which affirms the
following language: ‘I, (petitioner’s current full name), hereby attest under penalty of perjury that the request for a change in
gender to (female, male, or undesignated/non-binary) is to conform my legal gender to my gender identity and is not for any
fraudulent purpose.’”
