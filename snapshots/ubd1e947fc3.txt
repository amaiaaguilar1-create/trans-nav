LP VI Learner's Permit
To obtain a driver's license for the first time in the Virgin Islands, applicants must be at least 16 years old. The following steps outline the process for obtaining a Learner's Permit:
01
MEDICAL APPLICATION FORM
The first step in obtaining a Learner's Permit begins with completing the Medical/Application Form , which must be filled out and submitted by the applicant. The medical section of the form must be signed and stamped by a licensed local physician. This form is valid for one (1) year from the date of the signed exam. Applicants under the age of 18 must also submit a parent/guardian consent form , which must be notarized.
Resources: Application for First Time VI Driver's License Medical Form | First Time Applicant Driver's License Procedures |
02
KNOWLEDGE SKILLS EXAM
After submitting the medical/application form, applicants are required to take the Knowledge Skills Exam. The exam is administered by appointment only , and the applicable fee must be paid in advance. Applicants must present their completed medical/application form before taking the test. The written test will be administered in a private room with only the applicant and the BMV employee present. A passing grade of 75% or higher is required to proceed to the next step. To help prepare for the written test, applicants have the option to purchase the Driver's Manual , which contains essential information to help with studying. Additionally, a practice exam is available online, which can be used to further prepare for the test. If the applicant has difficulty reading or writing, oral exams may be provided at the discretion of the instructor. The oral exam option ensures that all applicants have a fair opportunity to complete the written test successfully.
Resources: Practice Written Exam for Learners Permit |
03
LEARNER'S PERMIT
Once the written test is completed successfully with a passing grade of 75% or higher, the applicant will be issued a Learner's Permit after the applicable fee is paid. Learner's permits are valid for six (6) months . If the applicant is under 18 years old, a parent or guardian must sign the learner's permit at the BMV office and provide legal documents that verify their relationship and identity. While driving, the applicant must carry both the medical/application form and the learner's permit at all times. Additionally, a licensed driver must accompany the applicant in the vehicle while they are driving.
04
ROAD TEST
An appointment for the road test must be made after passing the written test and paying the applicable fee. When scheduling the appointment , the applicant must present their completed medical/application form and learner's permit. On the day of the road test, only the applicant and the BMV inspector will be in the vehicle. The road test will be conducted without interference, and no one is allowed to enter the testing area besides the applicant and the inspector. The vehicle used for the road test must meet safety standards, including having an emergency brake between the driver and passenger seats. The vehicle owner must be present on the day of the test, and the applicant is required to dress appropriately—no short pants, revealing clothing, or slippers are allowed.
05
ISSUANCE OF DRIVER'S LICENSE
After successfully passing the road test, the applicant must select whether they want to apply/qualify for a REAL ID Driver’s License or a Limited Purpose Driver’s License . After selecting the appropriate option, the applicant will be issued their Driver’s License upon payment of applicable fees and verification of required documents. All applicants must provide the necessary legal documents for scanning and verification before receiving their driver's license.

Make an Appointment : The applicant must make an appointment for the application.

DUPLICATE Duplicate Driver's License
To obtain a duplicate copy of a Driver's License, follow the steps below:
Complete a Lost/Stolen/Destroyed Affidavit Form : The affidavit must be notarized.
License Verification : Your license will be verified and re-issued.
Designee Option : If the driver is unable to come in person, they may send a designee with the notarized affidavit. The affidavit must permit the designee authorization to obtain the duplicate license on their behalf.
Duplicate Fee : The duplicate fee is $55.
Late Fee : A late fee of $10 per month may apply if your renewal is overdue, with a maximum late fee of $250.

Proof of Address
US-VI Conversion of US State or Territory to a US Virgin Islands Driver’s License
To convert a driver’s license that was issued by a US State or Territory to a US Virgin Islands Driver’s License, follow the steps below:
Complete the Driver’s License Information Application Form : The form must be completely filled out by the applicant.
Surrender a Valid Unexpired Driver’s License : A valid unexpired driver’s license from the U.S. and/or U.S. jurisdiction must be surrendered.
License Verification : Your valid driver’s license will be verified with the issuing authority from the issuing state or jurisdiction.
Verification of Documents : For verification purposes, the documents listed in the legal documents/source documents section must be provided.
Driving History/Record : A driving history/record is required from the issuing state or jurisdiction within thirty (30) days of the application.
Conversion Fee : The fee for conversion is $55 , in addition to any late fees. A late fee of $10 per month may apply, with a maximum of $250 .
VI Driver’s License Issuance : A Virgin Islands driver’s license will be issued once all of the above requirements are turned in and verified.

Foreign-VI Conversion of a Foreign License to a US Virgin Islands Driver’s License
To convert a foreign driver’s license to a US Virgin Islands Driver’s License, follow the steps below:
Complete the Driver’s License Information Application Form : The form must be completely filled out by the applicant.
Show Legal Residency : The applicant must show legal residency in the U.S. Virgin Islands.
Present Legal Documents : All legal documents listed in the legal documents/source documents section must be presented.
Present a Valid Unexpired Driver’s License : A valid unexpired driver’s license must be presented for verification.
Verification of License : The Bureau of Motor Vehicles reserves the right to verify the license presented and check for any infractions with the issuing country.
Complete the Written Test : The written test is administered by appointment only.
Surrender of Foreign License : The valid unexpired driver’s license from the foreign country must be surrendered.
Payment of Fees : The conversion fee is $55, in addition to a $15 verification fee . After all requirements are satisfied and the applicable fees are paid, a US Virgin Islands Limited Purpose Driver’s License may be issued.
The Bureau of Motor Vehicles reserves the right to verify the license presented and check for any infractions with the issuing country.
