About Herpes Testing
If you think you may have herpes, herpes testing requires a different testing process that the Sexual Health Clinic provides. You will need to see one of our medical providers or gynecologists. To make an appointment with a medical provider or gynecologist, please call 410-837-2050 x4300.
Do you really need to get tested?
If you are sexually active, the answer is yes! STI testing is one of the most important things you can do to protect your health. Regular testing protects you and your partner(s).
But what if you use condoms every time?
Condoms are highly effective at preventing a lot of STIs – but they need to be used for every sex act. And some STIs – ones that are passed with skin-to-skin contact – may not be prevented with a condom.
Did you know that many STIs have no symptoms?
So even if you don’t see any symptoms of an STI it doesn’t mean you or your partner(s) don’t have an STI.
If there are no symptoms, does it matter?
Even without symptoms, infections can cause serious health problems and spread to others. Testing matters – once you know, you and your partner(s) can get the treatment you need.
Come in to see us and be open and honest; there's no shame in being a healthy sexual! We can help you learn which STI tests are right for you, help you understand and get the treatment if you need it, and give you options for prevention.
Is PrEP right for you?
If you are sexually active and HIV-negative, then yes! Being on PrEP will help put you in control of your sexual health! PrEP (pre-exposure prophylaxis) reduces your chances of getting HIV from sex or injection drug use. When taken as prescribed, PrEP is highly effective for preventing HIV.
How is PrEP taken?
PrEP is available as a pill and an injectable. We'll talk with you about the benefits of both so you can decide which is right for you.
Is PrEP safe?
Everyone has different experience with all medicines. But PrEP has a strong track record of people having little to no side effects and is extremely safe. We'll monitor you every step of the way and always be around to answer any questions you have.
