Sexual Health Clinic
Your sexual health matters - because sexual health IS health!
Our sex-positive team is here to help you get the screening, treatment, or protection you need so you can get back to getting it on!
Walk-ins Welcome
First come, first served. Or call for an appointment: 410-837-2050 ext. 4300
Come see us if you think you may have been exposed to a sexually transmitted infection (STI), have possible STI symptoms such as discharge, itching, or pain in your genital area, are interested in starting birth control or PrEP, or just want routine STI screening.
We offer:
STI screening and treatment
HIV Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP) and Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP)
DoxyPEP to prevent STI infections
Same-day hormonal birth control starts
Emergency pregnancy prevention
Free internal and external condoms, lube, tampons, and pads!
Herpes Testing
Note: If you suspect you may have herpes, because it requires a different testing process, you will need to see a medical provider or gynecologist. To make an appointment with a medical provider or gynecologist, please call 410-837-2050 x4300.
