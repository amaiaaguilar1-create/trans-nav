Our Team
Meet your primary care professionals at Diversity Family Health.
We pride ourselves on providing essential services to our patients in an discrimination-free environment. Our goal is to provide comprehensive care for the gender diverse and LGBTQ+ community, and improving your health.
Radona Hood
Provider
Radona Hood, ARNP-CNP, FNP-BC is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with a decade of clinical experience providing comprehensive, evidence-based care to patients across the lifespan. She is known for her patient-centered approach, strong clinical leadership, and commitment to improving health outcomes through education, prevention, and individualized treatment plans.
Radona earned her Master of Science in Nursing from Simmons College of Nursing and Health Sciences in Boston in 2015. Her clinical background is diverse and includes primary care, urgent care, maternal-fetal medicine, HIV care, sexual and reproductive health, hormone replacement therapy, and wellness services. She has extensive experience managing both chronic and acute conditions and is highly skilled in diagnostics, treatment planning, and preventive care.
Currently, Radona serves as the Nurse Practitioner at Diversity Family Health, Tulsa, where she oversees daily clinical operations and provides specialized care in HIV treatment and prevention, STI management, reproductive health, and contraception services. Throughout her career, she has also worked in military healthcare settings, high-risk obstetrics, telemedicine, wellness clinics, and aesthetic medicine. In addition to her clinical work, Radona is passionate about mentorship and has served as a preceptor for Family Nurse Practitioner students.
Radona is dedicated to creating a welcoming, respectful environment where patients feel heard, supported, and empowered to take an active role in their health.
Russell Rooms
Provider & Founder/CEO
Russell Rooms, DNP, APRN, FNP-BC , is a board certified Family Nurse Practitioner with over 30 years in the medical field and a Nurse Practitioner since 2004. He has a Master's degree from the University of Texas as an Emergency Clinical Nurse Specialist and a Post Masters degree as an Emergency Nurse Practitioner. Most recently Russell completed a Doctorate of Nursing Practice with a concentration in community and public health at Oklahoma City University where he focused on the prevention and treatment of anal cancers in men who have sex with men.
Russell specializes in conditions that effect the LGBTQ+ community with experience and training in gender affirming care, and HIV prevention and treatment. Russell also specializes in HPV related anal neoplasia and trained in high resolution anoscopy.
