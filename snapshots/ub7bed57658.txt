BAGLY Tea Time
BAGLY’s confidential, drop-in therapy session with a Licensed Independent Clinical Social Worker (LICSW). Appointments available in person and online every Wednesday from 3-7pm.

Short Term Therapy
BAGLY also offers short term therapy on an individual basis! Short term therapy includes 12-24 sessions - completely free.
MAKE FRIENDS AND FIND SUPPORT
Our social support services offer young people a chance to connect with new friends and trusted adults, explore their identities, and enjoy safe, fun events led by their peers.

The Health Education And Risk Reduction Team (HEARRT) is BAGLY’s youth-led health promotion group, working to change negative health outcomes within our community.
