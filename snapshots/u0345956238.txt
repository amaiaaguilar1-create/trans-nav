DPHSS Announces Temporary Limit on Vital Certificate Copies
Hagåtña, Guam – The Department of Public Health and Social Services (DPHSS) informs the
public that effective Tuesday, June 2, 2026, the Office of Vital Statistics will limit the issuance
of vital certificates to one (1) certified copy per event due to a delay in the replenishment of the
standard security paper required for printing vital records.
The temporary measure is intended to preserve available inventory and allow the Office of Vital
Statistics to continue providing certified copies to residents while awaiting the arrival of
additional supplies.
The department recognizes the importance of these documents to residents and appreciates the
public’s patience and understanding during this temporary situation. DPHSS remains proactive
in providing updates and will release further information as it becomes available.
For more information or clarification, contact the Office of Vital Statistics at (671) 300-9263 /
9264 / 9265 / 9270.
