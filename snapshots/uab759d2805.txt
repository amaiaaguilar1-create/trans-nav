Courthouse Instructions: The public entrance to the courthouse is on the east side of the building. A public parking lot is available on the corner of 9 th Street and 3 rd Avenue. The Sheriff, Veteran's Services, Auditor, Treasurer, Recorder, and County Administrator offices are located on the first floor. The Clerk's office is located on the second floor, and the State's Attorney's office is located on the fourth floor.
Jury Instructions: Enter through the public entrance and proceed through security. A sign and/or person will direct you to an assembly room for orientation.
Courtroom Rules: Electronic devices must be silenced. No food, drinks, or headwear (except for medical or religious purposes).
