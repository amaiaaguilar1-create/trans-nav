Court Fines & Fees
If you are ordered to fines, fees or child support through the Clerk of District Court office, you may make your payment in person, by mail, by phone, by fax or by email.
Our office is located at 200 West Broadway on the second floor of the courthouse. We accept cash, money order, and credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard and Discover). We do not accept personal checks, cashier’s checks or American Express credit cards.
Money orders
To expedite your payment, please remember to write your case number on your money order. If you would like a receipt, you will need to provide a self-addressed, stamped envelope.
You may mail money order payments to:
Clerk of District Court
200 West Broadway
Missoula, MT 59802
Credit and debit card
If paying by credit or debit card 1.995% plus a $1.25 processing fee will be added to your transaction.
If you choose to pay by credit or debit card, you may do so in person or by calling 406-258-4780. You will need to provide the following information:
Cardholder name
Card number
Expiration date
Billing zip code
