We’ve Got Your Back.
The Utah Pride Center (UPC) proudly offers programs and events that unite, empower and celebrate Utah's diverse LGBTQIA+ community.
Take Pride in Our Community
Your donation will directly support hundreds of LGBTQ+ individuals throughout the state of Utah and create spaces of safety, joy, and celebration of all identities. You can help support peer-led support groups, social groups, activities for children, families and seniors, plus education and trainings for allies!
