STD/HIV Programs
Sexual Health clinical services are provided by appointment only. A typical clinic visit costs $25 (cash only) and includes screening for HIV, Syphilis, Chlamydia and Gonorrhea. Call 603-624-6466 to make an appointment.
Presentations on STD/HIV prevention are available. For further information, please call (603) 624-6466.
