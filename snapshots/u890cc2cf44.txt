FLHSMV Gender Marker Changes on IDs and Licenses
Share this page
The ACLU is investigating rejected requests to change gender markers on state licenses and IDs. The Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (FLHSMV) released a technical advisory claiming that it will reject attempts to amend gender markers depending on what gender marker appears on supporting documentation.
