We are here to help you get the care you need. Find the health center nearest you, call 1-800-230-PLAN, or use the "Live Chat" feature in the bottom right corner (available during call center hours M-F, 8 am - 5 pm MT).
We accept most major forms of insurance, including Select Health, at all our health centers. However, some coverage depends on provider. Please call us if you need to confirm insurance coverage.

Beginning Monday, 1/12/2026, low-income Utahns who qualify can once again get no or very low-cost care at PPAU health centers for family planning services like birth control, STI testing and treatment, and cancer screenings.
No patients will be turned away because of an inability to pay.
Title X provides critical family planning services, such as affordable birth control, cancer screenings, and STI testing, to people who can’t otherwise afford these services on their own.
Call 1-800-230-PLAN to make an appointment today.
