Since 1989, the ChristianaCare Holloway Community Program has been providing compassionate, comprehensive care for individuals affected by AIDS. The Holloway Program is the leading HIV/AIDS care provider in Delaware, serving an estimated 65% of HIV-positive individuals in the state who are receiving medical care. We believe that access to treatment, education, and support is essential for improving health outcomes and enhancing quality of life. Through a multidisciplinary approach, we offer advanced medical therapies, counseling services designed to empower patients.
Our dedicated team include:
Board-certified infectious disease and internal medicine physicians
American Academy of HIV Medicine certified nurse practitioners
Pharmacists
Behavioral health prescribers
Phlebotomists
Social workers.
