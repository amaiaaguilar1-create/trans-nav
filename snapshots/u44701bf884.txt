Helping To End LGBTQ+ Young Adult Homelessness
Sweet Evening Breeze is committed to Louisville's LGBTQ+ young adults, 18 to 24 years of age, experiencing homelessness; by going beyond the expectations of a shelter, providing housing, hope, and healing.

What We Offer
We provide Louisville's LGBTQ+ young adults experiencing homelessness with an open and affirming environment, while connecting our residents to vital resources.

Ways To Get Involved
Intake Volunteer
Our intake volunteers are responsible for greeting clients at the intake window during operation hours.
Outreach Volunteer
Accompany us out in the community to assist our community members experiencing homelessness.
Assembly Volunteer
Our assembly volunteers help us with assembling care packages that will be used for community outreach.
