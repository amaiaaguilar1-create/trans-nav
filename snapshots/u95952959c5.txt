LGBTQIA+ Health
Our center is committed to providing a safe and affirming environment—no matter your gender identity or sexual orientation—with experience and sensitivity. We are proud to provide quality reproductive, sexual, and preventative healthcare to LGBTQIA+ patients.
At the Women’s Health Center of West Virginia, we strive to provide healthcare services to our LGBTQIA+ with expertise and sensitivity, including:
Gender-affirming hormone therapy
Annual wellness exams , including pelvic exams and chest and cervical cancer screenings
STI testing and treatment
HIV testing, treatment referral, and prevention with PrEP
Contraception and family planning
Emergency contraception (Plan B)
Our LGBTQIA+ Health Program complements the medical care we provide by offering
Medication Prior Authorizations (eg, support with insurance/pharmacy issues in securing medication)
Referrals for all types of health services (primary care, mental health, etc.)
National and community-based resources and information (including inclusive housing, businesses, etc.)
1-on-1 meetings to talk through questions, concerns, resources, etc.
Staff education
Name change and gender marker change resources & support
Free distribution of gender affirming gear
