Name/Gender Marker Change
We assist eligible individuals with name changes and gender marker changes. Getting a name change or gender marker change does not automatically change your name and gender marker with agencies like the DMV and Social Security Administration.
If you want to apply for help, we have walk-in hours Monday through Thursday between the hours of 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. Bring proof of income, a current ID, and a copy of your birth certificate (if available). You will complete an intake packet and meet with our legal advocates.
More information, guides, and forms for name changes are available on our Family Law Self-Help Center website .
