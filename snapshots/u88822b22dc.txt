Covered California
Covered California works to improve the health of all Californians by assuring their access to affordable, high quality care. Since opening its doors in 2014, Covered California has helped more than 2.9 million people buy health insurance.
This website contains resources for partners and information about the operations and governance of Covered California.
Consumers interested in learning more about enrolling in affordable health insurance should visit CoveredCA.com
