Trans Law Help Wisconsin, Inc.
‍ Your truth deserves recognition. Trans Law Help is here to offer trusted legal information on name and sex* marker changes in Wisconsin and to reduce individual barriers to affirmed identity. Because being yourself shouldn't be a legal challenge.
We are a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.
*Although the Wisconsin court forms use the word gender, we are using the word sex because that is the word the statute uses and because it is our belief that the requirement of a surgical sex change speaks to evidence of biological and hormonal sex, not gender identity.
