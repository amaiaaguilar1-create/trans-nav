Convenient Locations for Care Near You
Find a location near you! We have many sites to make getting care easy and close to home. Each location offers a wide range of services to meet your health needs.
We care for families across generations, with over 165,000 visits each year at 21 locations including 12 School Based Health Centers. As we grow to help more families and communities, we always focus on putting our patients first. Learn more about FHCHC today!
Our FHCHC Office Locations
Fair Haven Community Health Care brings quality care closer to you with multiple office locations throughout the community. Each facility is thoughtfully designed to offer a range of services, including medical, dental, and mental health care. Our dedicated teams are ready to provide compassionate, personalized care to help you and your family stay healthy.

Our School Based Health Centers
At Fair Haven Community Health Care, we care about your child's well-being, both in and out of the classroom. Our School-Based Health Centers provide accessible care for students, offering medical, mental, and preventive health services directly on school grounds. Our dedicated team is here to support your child's health and help them succeed every step of the way.
