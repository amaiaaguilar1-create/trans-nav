What to expect for a video visit?
It’s just like a regular doctor’s visit, only online or over the phone. If you’ve ever used FaceTime or Zoom, you’ll feel right at home.
Before your appointment, you’ll get a secure link by email (don’t forget to check your spam folder just in case). A nurse or staff member will check you in, ask a few quick questions, and get you ready to meet your provider. After your visit, they’ll check you out. Simple as that.
Who’s eligible for video visits?
Video visits are available to residents of North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and West Virginia .
You can schedule your appointment online or by calling 1-800-230-PLAN.

What to expect when using the PPDirect app?
You’ll choose the care you need in the PPDirect app, answer a few health questions, and submit your request with no appointment or video visit needed. A clinician will review your information and message you in the app if they have questions. If medication is prescribed, you may be able to have it delivered discreetly or pick it up at a local pharmacy, depending on the service and where you live.
Who is eligible to use the PPDirect app?
Eligibility for PPDirect depends on where you live, the service you’re requesting, and your health information .
Services vary by state, and a clinician reviews your answers to determine whether care through the app is appropriate. The app will show which services are available in your state.

What to expect for a MyChart message visit?
You’ll answer a series of medical questions through the MyChart portal, which will be reviewed by one of our clinicians. Our clinicians can diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, and create a personalized care plan for you. If a message visit isn’t the right fit medically, our team will help connect you to care through a video visit or an in-person appointment.
Who is eligible for MyChart message visits?
MyChart message visits are available to residents of North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and West Virginia.
To start your visit, you must have an active MyChart account. Once logged in, select e-visit to begin. If you do not yet have a MyChart account, call the health center nearest you to speak with health center staff for help getting set up.
