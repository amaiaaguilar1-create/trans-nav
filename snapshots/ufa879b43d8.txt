What It Means to Be an Academic Medical Center
The UNM Health Sciences Center is New Mexico&#8217;s only academic medical center and home to the state&#8217;s largest teaching hospital. Academic medical centers play a vital role in advancing healthcare by providing exceptional patient care, training the next generation of health professionals, and driving research that leads to innovative treatments and cures.
