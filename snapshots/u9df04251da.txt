Kevin Carbajal - RED Like Roses | November 7th
Join us for a gallery opening reception featuring contemporary landscape artist Kevin Carbajal!
A self-taught painter originally from Arizona, Kevin creates vibrant acrylic landscapes inspired by the natural world. Since moving to Oklahoma in 2021, he has explored the state’s diverse geography, transforming everyday scenery into expressive works that reflect human emotion, cultural identity, and personal experience.
His work has been featured at galleries throughout Oklahoma, including the Equality Center, KBM Art Gallery, MAINSITE Contemporary Art Gallery, and the Paseo Arts District.

Trans Day of Remembrance | November 20th
Join OKEQ for a night of reflection, remembrance, and resistance.
Trans individuals across the country are under attack for having the gall to live authentically.
Whether hatred from individual citizens or systematic discrimination, our brothers and sisters are being threatened daily with violence, micro-aggressions, and legislation aimed at erasure.
Every year we see nearly 50 deaths to violence or suicide within the trans community and it is important that we take time to remember those losses as well as fight to prevent future losses.
OKEQ is observing the national Trans Day of Remembrance on Friday, November 20 with stories of survival, a reading of names lost over the past few years, and commissioned performance by local trans artist and activist, Jordan Wright.
Proceeds from this event support OKEQ’s Health Clinic which specializes in gender affirming care.
Tickets start at $10

Oklahomans for Equality seeks equal rights for 2SLGBTQIA+ individuals and families through intersectional advocacy, education, programs, alliances, and the operation of the Dennis R. Neill Equality Center.
Serving the community and allies since 1980, Oklahomans for Equality is home to a diverse, vibrant, and engaged community, knowledgeable staff, and dedicated volunteers.
