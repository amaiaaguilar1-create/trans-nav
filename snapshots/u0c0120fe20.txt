July 25, 2023
Families and Medical Providers Sue Missouri to Block Transgender Youth Medical Care Ban
A lawsuit to block the implementation of SB 49 was filed in Cole County Circuit Court, which would prevent transgender adolescents in Missouri from accessing evidence-based gender-affirming medical care. The challenge to SB 49 was filed on behalf of three families of transgender young people, medical providers, and the organizations PFLAG and GLMA by Lambda Legal, the ACLU of Missouri, and Bryan Cave Leighton Paisner LLP. The law is scheduled to take effect on August 28, 2023.
History
July 25, 2023: Lambda Legal files lawsuit.
August 2023: Court denied the motion for a preliminary injunction
August 28, 2023: SB 49 took effect
September 23, 2024: Trial commenced
October 3, 2024: Two-week trial concluded
November 25, 2024: Court issued decision upholding SB 49
January 16, 2025: Appeal to the Missouri Supreme Court was filed
September 24, 2025: Missouri Supreme Court heard oral arguments to reverse a circuit court ruling upholding enforcement of SB 49
January 13, 2026: Missouri Supreme Court upheld SB 49, allowing the ban to remain in effect
