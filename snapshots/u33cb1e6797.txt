Who is SC Thrive?
We envision stabilized, healthy communities. For all South Carolinians.
That means your physical health is thriving. Your basic needs are met. Your mental wellbeing is prioritized. Your financial outlook is trending upward. From you to your aging grandparent to your neighbor’s newborn baby to the stranger you saw on the street corner. Everyone should thrive. And we won’t stop until you do.

PARTNERS
Building Connections
SC Thrive connections are changing lives across our state.
View More
Through technology, we link people seamlessly to state agencies and organizations, simplifying the benefits process for those in need.
From food resources to financial support, healthcare access to mental wellness resources, elder care and more, we cut through the clutter to connect people to crucial benefits.
Through training, we equip professionals to help others better.
With knowledge and education opportunities highlighting state and federal programs, mental health response, community case management and more, our partners leave with a more empathetic approach and deeper understanding of what many South Carolinians face.
Through connections, we provide access and build trust.
SC Thrive is a conduit and facilitator: creating working relationships between businesses, religious groups, governmental agencies, healthcare entities, and nonprofit organizations.
