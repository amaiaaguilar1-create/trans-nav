Mental Health, Autism, and Substance Use Disorder Treatment Parity
Pennsylvania has the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA) that requires mental health and substance use disorder parity, which means that your health insurance company is required by law to provide you with the same level of health insurance coverage for mental health, autism services, and substance abuse disorder treatment as you would have for medical/surgical services.<br>\r\n</p>\r\n<p><a href=\&#34;/content/copapwp-pagov/en/agencies/insurance/laws-regulations-notices/mental-health-parity.html\&#34; target=\&#34;_self\&#34;>Learn more about your health insurance coverage for mental health services, autism services, and substance use disorder treatment.</a></p>\r\n&#34;}}">
Pennsylvania has the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act (MHPAEA) that requires mental health and substance use disorder parity, which means that your health insurance company is required by law to provide you with the same level of health insurance coverage for mental health, autism services, and substance abuse disorder treatment as you would have for medical/surgical services.
