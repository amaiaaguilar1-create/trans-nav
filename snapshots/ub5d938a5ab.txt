Supreme Court Issues Order Changing Fees for Court Filings and Record Checks
Fee Increases to Take Effect July 1st
Concord, NH – The New Hampshire Supreme Court today issued an order .pdf archive amending a number of rules related to fees for court filings and record checks. As a result, various court fees will be increasing, including those for case initiation and other filings. This is the first time court filing fees have changed since 2013. The fee structure for court record checks is also changing from the current structure of $20 per name for the first five names, and $5 for each additional name, to a flat fee of $6.50 per name. The fee increases will take effect on Tuesday July 1, 2025, the first day of the new biennium. A complete list of the fee changes is available by viewing today’s Supreme Court order .pdf archive online.
