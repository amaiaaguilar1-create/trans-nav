Application for Renewal or Duplicate of Alabama Driver License or ID card
Alabama residents unable to visit an issuing office and individuals who are out of state for one of the reasons listed below
may complete the following information to request a renewal or duplicate of their Driver License or Non-driver ID by mail,
if they have obtained an Alabama Driver License / ID card with their picture and signature in the last four years (some
exceptions may apply). A Renewal License may be applied for up to 180 days prior to current expiration date.

Please Note:
• Star ID cannot be added by mail. First issuances of a STAR ID require you to visit an ALEA Driver License Office.
• To update the name on your license/ID: Include photocopies of legal name change documentation and the updated social security card.
• Foreign Nationals must include front and back photocopies of all authorized presence documents. To remove the FN designation, you must provide
either a photocopy of the Certificate of Naturalization or US Passport. FN applications will be processed based on the time returned by SAVE, which may
or may not match your submitted presence documents.
• To add the Motorcycle endorsement to your license for the first time, you must include a photocopy of your Motorcycle Safety Foundation Basic
RiderCourse Card (plus additional $5.00 fee).
• If a boating certificate is found in the system for you, the Vessel Class will automatically be added to your license and an additional $5.00 will be owed.
