We Can Help
The Philadelphia Bar Association Lawyer Referral and Information Service (LRIS) has been successfully matching clients with carefully screened and well qualified lawyers for more than 70 years. You can put your trust in us to find the right lawyer for you.
If you cannot afford to hire a lawyer, our staff may be able to refer you to a legal aid agency or other resource that may be able to help you for low or no fee.
