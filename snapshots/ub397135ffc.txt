Duplicate Driver's License or Identification Card ($11.00):
An individual needing either a duplicate driver’s license or identification card may order one on-line .
If you need your driver's license or identification card immediately, you must appear in person at any Driver Service Bureau station . Customers who visit a Driver Service Bureau Station for a duplicate driver's license or identification card are required to take a new photograph and complete the duplicate application .
The Driver Service Bureau highly recommends all applicants make an appointment at dps.ms.gov/appointment .
