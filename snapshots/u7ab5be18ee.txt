ORDER SEALING FINAL ORDER TO CHANGE NAME
THIS MATTER comes to the Court's attention as a result of Petitioner’s Final Order
to Change Name. The Court has determined, sua sponte, that the Final Order to Change Name, filed
concurrently with this Order, shall be sealed pursuant to Rule 1-079 NMRA. The Court finds that
the Final Order to Change Name contains protected personal identifier information. An overriding
interest exists in maintaining the confidentiality of an individual’s personal information that
overcomes the right of public access to the court record and which supports sealing the Order to
protect confidentiality. A substantial probability exists that this overriding interest will be
prejudiced if the court record is not sealed. This proposed sealing is narrowly tailored and, at this
time, no less restrictive means exist to achieve the confidentiality of the Final Order filed in the
Court.
This Order is not sealed, and Petitioner in this matter is authorized to have access to the
sealed Court record. This Order shall remain in effect until further order of the Court. Petitioner is
entitled to notice of any future motion to unseal the court record or modify the sealing Order.
IT IS SO ORDERED.
