Substance Use Support Available at HOPES
At Northern Nevada HOPES, our MAT program offers caring, team-based treatment to give you hope and improve your life as you work toward recovery. We accept Medicaid, most major health plans, and, for those without insurance, we have a sliding fee scale that adjusts cost based on what you can afford. If you’re interested in receiving medication-assisted treatment, the first step is to become a HOPES patient by calling 775-786-4673.
