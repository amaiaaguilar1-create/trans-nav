Nationwide – including all across the South – it is legal and possible for transgender adults to access gender-affirming care. However, trans adults in the South can face additional challenges or barriers to accessing care.
Trans in the South: A Directory of Trans-Affirming Health & Service Providers, is a resource to help transgender adults in Southern states identify healthcare and other service providers who are affirming and competent in serving the transgender community.
You’ll find lists of trans-friendly mental health providers, primary care physicians, HIV care specialists, attorneys, endocrinologists, and more across 13 Southern states (Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, West Virginia). In addition to a virtual, searchable directory, we&#8217;ve also compiled resources on topics such as funding medical transition and telehealth.
We hope this resource guide helps you access the services you need and deserve.
DISCLAIMER: This resource provides information about acquiring medical or attorney referrals; it is not medical or legal advice. Moreover, due to the rapidly changing nature of the law and our reliance on information provided by outside sources, we make no warranty or guarantee.
Starting Your Search
We encourage you to use the Trans in the South guide as a starting point for your research – but to please be in touch with providers directly to understand which services are available. The Campaign for Southern Equality strives to keep the Trans in the South guide up-to-date, but we also understand that some medical providers in the region are modifying their offerings because of anti-transgender laws or policies that have been passed or introduced. We send support as you navigate this challenging time, and we commit to working with partners across the country to increase access to care.
Get Support Navigating Access in NC & TN
As part of our Trans in the South Access Project , we are piloting navigation support services for trans adults in Tennessee and North Carolina, where disruptions to care have been especially pronounced. If you have experienced an interruption to care in TN or NC, please fill out the intake form – and we’ll connect you with an Access Navigator who can help you understand your options to continue care and information related to care. Financial support is not available through this project.

Seeking Providers for Trans Youth?
Access to gender-affirming care for transgender youth has become increasingly challenging in recent years. If you are the parent or caregiver of a trans young person who needs help understanding the care landscape, please reach out to our Trans Youth Emergency Project . TYEP is a national project providing family navigation services, renewable $500 travel grants, and more to support families of trans youth in accessing safe, legal gender-affirming care.
