Apply for Medicaid
In 2024, more than 1 million Alabama citizens qualified for Medicaid benefits through a variety of programs for children, families and pregnant women as well as for elderly and/or disabled people.
Each Medicaid program has its own requirements you must meet in order to qualify for benefits. All programs have income requirements. Some programs also have age requirements or are limited to certain groups such as pregnant women or people who also have Medicare. To learn more about the requirements for different Medicaid programs, click on one of the links at right or below.
Qualifying for Medicaid
Meet all income, age or other requirements
Fill out all forms correctly and completely
Apply online or turn your application form into the right office or worker
For more help, call the Recipient Call Center (toll-free) at (800) 362-1504, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. until 4:30 p.m.
