Diane & Bruce Halle Arizona Burn Center
Established in 1965, the Diane & Bruce Halle Arizona Burn Center is one of the largest civilian burn units in the nation. Serving the entire southwestern U.S., it is a comprehensive facility that provides the newest research, technologies and products for treatment of burn-related injuries. It also provides treatment for complicated skin infections and disorders.
