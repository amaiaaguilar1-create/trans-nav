Disclaimer
The Virginia Transgender Resource & Referral List (“the List”) is intended as an informational resource
only. Providers included here have not been endorsed, certified, or vetted by the Virginia Department
of Health beyond fulfilling the requirement to complete and send in the Transgender Resource and
Referral List Information Form. VDH can make no guarantees about providers listed here. VDH
contacts all entities included on the List annually to check that each organization’s information is
accurate and up to date, but cannot guarantee accuracy throughout the year.
Further, websites and other materials developed or produced by these providers are solely the
responsibility of those entities. VDH cannot censor external sites’ content, so it is possible that some
individuals who choose to browse an outside website may encounter materials or information that they
consider offensive or inappropriate. If you have questions or comments about content on a provider’s
website, please contact the provider directly to address your concerns.
Additional Resources
For additional transgender health resources, visit the Transgender Health Services and support
webpage at https://bit.ly/3myA6YJ.
For questions or resources about HIV, STIs, or viral hepatitis, contact staff at the Virginia Disease
Prevention Hotline at (800) 533-4148.
If you need resources that are not included in the List, you are encouraged to contact Ted Heck at
ted.heck@vdh.virginia.gov or 804-845-1722.

Services Provided: HSZ&W is a statewide 501(c)3 non-profit offering weekly support meetings for
parents, caregivers, and adult family members of transgender and gender-diverse individuals.

(virtual and in-person) 10+ meetings every month for parents, caregivers, and adult family

gatherings for school age kids and their families; and Trans Adult Social Club (TASC), offered in

also offers a Trans Wellness Fund (see website links above) to help bridge the gap to access to
gender affirming care and Ally & Community Education, a professional development training
program for schools, businesses, agencies, and community groups wanting to learn more about
gender diversity and how to create inclusive spaces fostering a sense of belonging and wellbeing.
Visit our website (see above) to fill out an inquiry based interest form then follow the emailed
directions to set up a personal welcome call. Any questions, reach out to Shannon McKay,
Executive Director.
