About Hudson Pride Center
Who We Are
SAFE. AFFIRMING. SUPPORTIVE. INCLUSIVE.
We’re the largest LGBTQ+ social services center in New Jersey, and we offer a variety of free services to support, affirm and connect our community to each other and to the services they need. While Hudson Pride Center was founded by a group of visionary activists in 1993 with a focus on the safety and wellbeing of Hudson County’s LGBTQ+ residents who were affected by the AIDS epidemic, it is growing to meet the current needs of our entire community.

Offering Helping Hands
Health Services Support
Support is a core principle at Hudson Pride Center. It is the motivation behind all of our services and has been a source of success in creating real and positive change. Mental and physical health are key quality-of-life factors. To that end, we do all we can to ensure the members of our community have access to a multitude of services and that neither financial status nor native language are a barrier.

Social Services & Activities
A sense of belonging ranks among the top five basic human needs. In other words, life is better together, so Hudson Pride Center has created support groups, workshops, events and other activities that allow and encourage LGBTQ+ people to connect, commune and focus on collective empowerment.
