For any transaction taking place at a PennDOT Driver License Center, payment cards as well as checks or money orders made payable to the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania are the only acceptable forms of payment.
If you are coming to our Riverfront Office Center location in Harrisburg, payment cards, cash, check and money orders are the only acceptable forms of payment. Please make checks or money orders payable to the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania .
Online transactions may be paid for using either a credit card or a debit card. Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover are the only forms of credit or debit cards accepted online.
Any applications requiring a fee and being sent through the mail to PennDOT, must have a check or money order accompanying them. Please have the check or money order made payable to the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
