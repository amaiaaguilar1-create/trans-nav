Variety Care sites utilize Champions
who serve as allies connecting
members of the 2sLGBTQ+ community
to health care services.
In 2019, Variety Care expanded primary care services offered t patients by including HIV screenings in annual exams and health checkups. Services also include HIV prevention (PrEP + DoxyPEP) and treatment.
Uninsured and underinsured patients may qualify to pay for services on a sliding fee scale.
To schedule an appointment, call (405) 632-6688 or visit their website to learn more.
