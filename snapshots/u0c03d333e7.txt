AMENDED ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER
Re: Court Costs arid Sheriff's Fees Effective July 1, 2021
The St. Louis City Circuit Court converted to E-Filing on July 8, 2013. The new deposit
amounts to secure court costs (filing fees) for the 22nd Judicial Circuit, as modified beginning
July 1, 2021, shall be as follows:
Associate Civil Cases $93.50
Sheriff's service fee for each
Defendant to be served by the City Sheriff 36.00
Circuit Civil Cases 145.50
Sheriff's service fee for each
Defendant to be served by the City Sheriff 36.00
Family Court Matters 177.50
Sheriff's service fee for Respondent
To be served by City Sheriff 36.00
Small Claims Cases (under $5,000) 80.50
Sheriff's service fee for each City defendant 36.00
Certified Mail service fee for each defendant 10.04
Expungement (Expunge Record 610.140) 395.50 plus service
Trials de Novo 45.00
Appeals 70.00
Garnishments 10.00
When paying filing fees for any particular case, the attorney should include an amount
for service only when that attorney is requesting service by the St. Louis City Sheriffs Office.
Payment for the cost of service by the sheriff of any other county or by a private process server
should be made directly to the server of process and should not be included in the costs deposit
paid to the St. Louis City Circuit Clerk.
These filing fees shall be posted on the Court's web site (www.sticitycircuitcourt.cilin)
pursuant to Local Rule 5.1.
