Contact Us
The Legal Aid Society of Cleveland serves Ashtabula, Cuyahoga, Geauga, Lake, and Lorain Counties through numerous offices and outreach sites. Need help? Apply for free legal help online or call 888-817-3777 to begin an intake application by phone.
Call Us With Your Quick Question
We offer two phone lines for quick questions related to your housing and economic justice problems. If your issue is more than a quick question, you can complete an intake for further legal help.
Tenant Info Line: 216-861-5955
Economic Justice Info Line: 216-861-5899
