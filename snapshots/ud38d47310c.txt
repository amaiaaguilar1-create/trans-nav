LGBTQ+ Healthcare
Evergreen specializes in the health and wellness needs of LGBTQ+ patients. We provide affirming medical care and supportive services, led by your individual needs and goals.
If you're looking for a provider who respects and understands you, get in touch with our team today to establish care with us.
Evergreen's services for LGBTQ+ patients include:
Primary care and Specialty care
Medical Case Management
Referrals to specialists
Referrals to affirming pelvic health and gynecology providers
Screening and treatment for HIV, hepatitis C, and STIs
Access to PrEP (medication for HIV prevention) and PEP (medication taken after exposure to prevent HIV)
We can also support you with:
Referrals to culturally competent mental health services
Assistance navigating the legal system
Harm reduction and substance use services
Free condoms and lube
