Name Change
Disclaimer: Court staff is not permitted to provide legal advice. To receive legal
advice, you should contact a licensed attorney. The phone number for the
Philadelphia Bar Association Legal Referral and Information Service is 215-238-
6333. Nothing in this publication is meant to be construed as legal advice.
1. A petition for name change is filed at the Prothonotary’s Office, City Hall, room
287. The hours of operation are 8:30am to 5:00pm.
2. Before you file a petition you must be fingerprinted. Bring your fingerprint cards,
birth certificate, social security card, and a valid photo I.D. with you when filing
your petition.
3. After filing the petition your case will be scheduled before a hearing officer.
Within 30 days of the hearing date you must obtain a judgment search from
Family Court at 34 South 11th Street, Philadelphia, PA 19107, and the
Prothonotary’s Office at City Hall, room 262. You must also publish notice of
your name change in the Philadelphia Daily News (215-854-2000) and Legal
Intelligencer (215-551-2300). There are additional fees for publication.
4. At your hearing bring the results of the judgment search and proof of publication.
