STD/HIV/Viral Hepatitis (SHVH) Program
Overview
The STD/HIV/Viral Hepatitis Program promotes prevention, education, awareness, outreach, and linkage to care related to sexually transmitted diseases, HIV, and viral hepatitis.
The program supports public health initiatives that encourage prevention strategies, reduce transmission risks, and increase community awareness regarding testing, education, and available support resources.
Program Activities
Community education campaigns
Prevention outreach initiatives
Public awareness presentations
Risk reduction education
Referral and linkage support
Educational material distribution
Community partnership activities
Health promotion events
Outreach to priority populations
Public health collaboration efforts
Areas of Focus
Prevention Education
The program promotes awareness of prevention strategies that support healthier behaviors and reduce transmission risks.
Early Awareness and Testing
Educational outreach encourages awareness of available health resources and preventive services.
Community Engagement
The program works closely with organizations and community partners to strengthen outreach and public education efforts.
Public Health Support
SHVH initiatives contribute to Guam’s broader efforts to improve community health and reduce infectious disease impacts.
Program Goals
Reduce transmission risks
Increase public awareness and education
Promote prevention and wellness strategies
Strengthen community outreach efforts
Improve access to educational resources
Support healthier communities
Community Impact
The STD/HIV/Viral Hepatitis Program strengthens public health awareness through education, outreach, and prevention initiatives that support healthier communities across Guam.
