Mission
The Annex helps young people take charge of their whole health by providing equitable, accessible, and confidential sexual health services and education .
The Annex is Temporarily Closed due to Flooding
We are sad to report that the Annex will be closed the week of November 9th due to a cracked pipe causing significant flooding on both floors of the clinic. Our staff are currently working very hard to recude damages at the clinic and think creatively about how we can return to providing young people in our community with care as safely and as quickly as possible.
If you would like to support the Annex as we figure out next steps, you can donate to our GiveMN page . We are very grateful to have matching grants in effect which means that your donations will go even further to help the clinic during this difficult time.
Please stay tuned as we provide updates here and on our social media regarding the state of the clinic and the availability of services. We thank you for your patience at this time.
