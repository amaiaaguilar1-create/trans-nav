The Annex is Open!
While the Annex had to temporarily close due to flooding on November 9th, we have resumed clinical services! Patients can schedule appointments by texting or calling 763.533.1316 during our normal business hours and will come to their appointment by entering through the back staff entrance, where a member of our team will be waiting to get them checked in and prepared for their visit.
Because of damage caused by flooding, the waiting room is under construction, so patients need to visit the back door and ring the doorbell to be seen. If you have any questions or concerns you can reach out to 763.533.1316.
People who would like to help us invest in the repairs needed to fix the issues caused by the flooding can donate to our GiveMN page . Thank you!
