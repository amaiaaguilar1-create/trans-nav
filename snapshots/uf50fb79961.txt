Costs and Fees
The costs and fees for vital records are non-refundable and non-transferable, even if the record is not found or is identified incorrectly.
Note: For in-person orders at DSHS in Austin, payment made by credit card requires a $2.25 processing fee.
The expedited service fee, which shortens processing time, has changed from $5 to $25 to cover the costs for the service. Please note the expedited service fee is to expedite our application processing, and does not guarantee that the requested record will be provided if all applications requirements are not met. For expedited service, the application must be mailed to us via an overnight mail service.

Expedited Processing and Shipping
To request EXPEDITED service, you must:
Send your complete application packet (including the application with a notary seal, any required supporting documentation, and payment for your application) through an overnight mail service, such as FEDEX, LoneStar, or UPS;
Send the order to the Expedited Processing Mailing Address ;
Select and provide payment for one of the expedited return shipping methods:
Expedited Return Shipping Method Cost
EXPEDITED – Overnight Mail (shipping within USA ONLY) $16.00
EXPEDITED – USPS Express Mail (shipping overnight to PO Box ONLY) $22.95
Provide expedited processing fee*:
Expedited Processing Fee Cost
Expedited Processing Fee $25.00
*You can request overnight return shipping without an expedited processing; however, if expedited processing is selected, expedited return shipping is required.
