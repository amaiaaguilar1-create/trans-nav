Portland Regional Office
Serving Clackamas, Hood River, Multnomah, Sherman, and Wasco counties
Intake is currently only available by phone and we are unable to take walk-ins.
Legal Aid will make arrangements for interpreters for other limited English speakers. If you need an interpreter or use a TTD, call (503) 224-4086 , and ask for an interpreter in your native language. Our staff has access to on-demand interpretation services.
The Portland Regional office offers full legal representation, brief advice, Pro Se Legal Clinics, and Self-Help Materials for the following legal areas
Family Law for Survivors of Domestic and Sexual Violence
Protection Orders for Abuse Survivors
Immigration for Abuse Survivors
Public Benefits
Housing Law for Tenants
Other legal matters
