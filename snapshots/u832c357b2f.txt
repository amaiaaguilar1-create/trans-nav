Most patients visit our clinic in Waikiki to receive comprehensive primary care services, including behavioral health, social services, health counseling and pharmacy services.
Appointments
Patients should call ahead to schedule an appointment. Same day appointments may be available.
Payment Information
Medicaid, Medicare and most private insurance plans are accepted. A sliding fee scale is available for patients without insurance. Please speak with a patient service representative for further details.
Parking
Free parking is available on the Ohua side parking lot. A parking pass can be picked up at the reception desk.
