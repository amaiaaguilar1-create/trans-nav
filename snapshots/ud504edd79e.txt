Gender Reassignment
Authorization
16 Admin. Code, 4205, Subsection 10.6 – Legal Change of Name. and Subsection 10.7 – Amendment of Sex.
Who May Request a Gender Reassignment
• Registrant, if registrant is 18 years of age or older.
• For minor (under 18 years of age) - one of the parents, legal guardian, or a legal
representative.
Required Documents
☐ Requester’s Affidavit for Sex Change on Birth Certificate - must be signed and
notarized.
☐ Healthcare Provider’s Affidavit for Sex Change on Birth Certificate – must be
signed and notarized.
☐ If there is a name change - must include a certified copy of a court order.
☐ Valid photo identification card of registrant (or) if a minor, valid photo identification
card of parent, legal guardian, or a legal representative.
☐ The original certified birth certificate must be surrendered to the Office of Vital
Statistics before a new birth certificate will be released.
☐ If the registrant does not have an existing birth certificate, a $25 payment in check or
money order is required.
Where to Submit
Office of Vital Statistics
Jesse Cooper Building
417 Federal Street
Dover, DE 19901
Processing Time
• Processing time will be within two weeks.
