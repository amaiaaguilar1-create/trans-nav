How do I start gender-affirming hormone therapy?
You can start your male-to-female (MTF) or female-to-male (FTM) gender transition with gender-affirming hormone therapy. You start by speaking with a licensed clinician who understands your transition goals and will review your medical history. This may include discussing hormone therapy, mental health support, or other aspects of care.
With Plume, you can start gender-affirming hormones online. No therapist letter or insurance required!
How do I get a gender-affirming hormone therapy prescription online?
You can get a gender-affirming hormone therapy prescription online by meeting virtually with a licensed clinician who is qualified to prescribe treatment.
Plume provides gender-affirming hormone therapy through secure online appointments. After signing up, you’ll meet with a licensed clinician who will guide you through your options and create a personalized care plan based on your needs. If hormone therapy is appropriate for you, your clinician can prescribe medication and arrange any necessary lab work without requiring an in-person visit*.
*Florida law requires informed consent forms to be signed in person to receive treatment. Learn more here.
What falls under gender affirming care?
Gender-affirming care includes medical and supportive services that help people align their physical characteristics and overall well-being with their gender identity.
This can include hormone therapy such as testosterone or estrogen, mental health support, lab monitoring, and ongoing guidance from experienced clinicians. Gender-affirming care can also include services like voice support, hair removal, and surgical referrals, depending on your goals. The specific care you receive depends on your needs and what feels right for you.
What is Plume, and how does it work?
Plume is an online gender-affirming care clinic that provides medical care for trans and nonbinary people through licensed clinicians.
Members can access trans care entirely online, including consultations, prescriptions, and ongoing support. After joining, you’ll meet with a clinician who will create a personalized care plan and continue supporting you throughout your gender transition.
Is Plume a legitimate clinic?
Yes. Plume is a legitimate healthcare provider offering gender-affirming care through licensed clinicians specialized in transgender healthcare. All care is delivered by qualified medical professionals who follow established clinical guidelines. Plume provides ongoing support, prescriptions, and lab coordination through a secure online platform.
Are Plume providers licensed doctors?
Yes. Plume clinicians are licensed healthcare professionals, including doctors and nurse practitioners. They are experienced in gender-affirming care and are qualified to prescribe hormone therapy, order lab tests, and provide ongoing medical support.
