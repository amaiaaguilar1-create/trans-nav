New Online Portal
Meet myDrive
Kentucky’s New Home for Online Driver Services
The Kentucky Transportation Cabinet (KYTC) is upgrading systems and launching myDrive , a new portal for managing your driver license and ID services online.
Beginning June 12
Full-day walk-in services will resume at all Driver Licensing Regional Office locations.
Account Verification
To access online services, you’ll need to create an account and link it to your Kentucky credential. Customers will need a unique Letter ID alphanumeric code from a KYTC document dated on or after June 6, 2026, issued within the last 60 days to connect your account to your credential.
Don’t have one? Request a Letter ID by mail when creating your myDrive account or visit a Driver Licensing Regional Office. Delivery typically takes 5–10 business days .

Update or Replace a Driver's License or Permit
Address Change
If you have moved, you must update the address on your Kentucky credential within 10 days. Before updating your address with the Kentucky Transportation Cabinet, update your mailing address with the United States Postal Service first to help ensure your new card is mailed to the correct address.
Update Your Address
The fee to update the address on a credential is $15.00.
Eligible customers may be able to update their address online within their verified myDrive account, by mail, or in person at any Regional office.
One Acceptable Proof Document PDF is required when updating your address.

Name Change
If your legal name has changed, you must update the name on your Kentucky credential to match your current legal documents. Name changes must be completed in person at a Driver Licensing Regional Office and require supporting legal documentation.
Update Your Name
Update your name with the Social Security Administration
You must update your name with the Social Security Administration before applying for a new driver’s license or permit. Your information must match SSA records before your application can be processed at a Regional Office.
Gather your name change documents
Bring your updated Social Security card and legal documentation PDF showing your name change, such as a marriage certificate, divorce decree, court order, or other approved legal name change document.
Complete a pre-application on myDrive, if desired
Completing a pre-application through myDrive can help ensure you bring the correct documents and save time during your visit to a Regional Office.
Visit a Driver Licensing Regional Office
Name changes must be completed in person at a Regional Office. A $15.00 fee applies.

Frequently Asked Questions
Open all
Close all
Can I update my address online?
Yes. Eligible customers may update their address online through myDrive. A connected account is required, and valid proof of your new Kentucky address must be uploaded.
Can I change my name online or by mail?
No. Name changes must be completed in person and require supporting legal documentation.
Can I order a replacement online?
Yes. Eligible customers may request a replacement online through myDrive. A connected account is required.
Do I need to bring all required REAL ID documents when requesting a replacement REAL ID?
No. If all information on your current REAL ID is up to date, no documentation is required. If you need to update your name or address, you will need to provide supporting documents.
Is a vision screening required to update information on my credential?
No. A vision screening is required only if you are within your renewal window and choose to renew at the same time.
How long will it take to receive my new credential?
Once processed, you will receive a temporary document. Your permanent credential typically arrives by mail within 10–15 business days.
Will I receive a temporary credential?
Yes. Customers receive a temporary credential while waiting for the permanent card to arrive by mail.
Am I required to fill out a pre-application online before visiting a Regional Office?
No. Completing a pre-application through myDrive is not required to visit an office, but it can help ensure you bring the correct documents and save time during your visit.
