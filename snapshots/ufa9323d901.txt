New Look. Same Values.
Our mission is to lead the fight to eliminate HIV in Delaware by championing HIV care and prevention services. For 30 years, we’ve worked to help people with HIV thrive. The work is essential, so we continue to push our mission forward. That’s why we’ve refreshed our look. We need a brand that shows our dedication to change and momentum. A brand we can all get behind to fight for an HIV-free future. We never forget that we’re fighting for real people. And we’re just getting started.
