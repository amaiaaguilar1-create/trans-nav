The Six Circles of Care
Six interconnected programs holding the whole person: body, mind, community, and soul. Pick a doorway. Every one leads home.
Youth & Young Adults
Hero Academy leadership, Pride Lounge at Supercon, Pride Scholarships, and sexual health education. This circle is designed for ages 13–24, including neurodivergent and nerd-culture communities.

Why we exist
Care for the whole person: creative, bold, and unapologetically ours.
That conviction has carried Pridelines from a circle of chairs in 1982 to an ecosystem of care for every generation across Miami-Dade County. Everyone means everyone.
Whether you’re a teenager attending your first youth hangout or a long-term HIV survivor navigating aging with dignity, every program is designed to help you live authentically, maintain healthy boundaries, and build the connections that matter most.
This model is built on the conviction that EVERYONE deserves the same quality of integrated, whole-person care delivered through a community center that is creative, bold, and unapologetically committed to our national motto, &#8220;E Pluribus Unum,&#8221; which means &#8220;Out of Many, One.&#8221;

The Third Weekend
Every month, one back-to-back weekend of food, performance, and community: the market by day, the Lounge by night, and Sunday to close it out. Come for one, stay for all three.
All Events
New Eden Jubilee Market
Third Saturdays · 2:00–5:30 PM
A free monthly farmers market at the Unitarian Universalist Congregation of Miami. Fresh food, community partners, radical welcome.
The Lavender Lounge
Third Saturdays · 7:00–10:30 PM
A monthly evening at UU Miami where the format rotates and the welcome stays constant: theatre, drag, live music, and dance nights. Food is always free.
QU33RDO Events
Third Sunday · 7:00–11:00 PM
Nerd culture, cosplay, and fandom take over Lincoln’s Beard Brewing Co., presented with King Vyper Entertainment.
