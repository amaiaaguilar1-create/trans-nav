The Second Judicial Circuit is the largest judicial circuit in South Dakota by population with about 285,467 residents, according to the latest census figures. The Circuit Administration office is located in the Minnehaha County Courthouse in Sioux Falls.

Traffic Notice
The reconstruction of Minnesota Avenue from Eighth Street to Brookings Avenue in Sioux Falls is affecting traffic access to the Minnehaha County Courthouse. Minnesota Avenue has been reduced to two lanes, and vehicles cannot access the courthouse from Minnesota Avenue. The traveling public is advised to approach the Minnehaha County Courthouse from the east or by using Dakota Avenue or Main Avenue. Road construction may also affect parking around the courthouse. The public is advised to allow for extra time when driving to the courthouse.
