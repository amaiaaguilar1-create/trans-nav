Gender Affirming Care
Gender-Affirming Care at Equitas Health goes beyond meds and hormone therapy. Your personal journey starts with a compassionate and skilled care team at your side.
Make an Appointment
Your Gender-Affirming Care Team
Our Gender-Affirming Care model is built on freedom and fairness. You have the freedom to name what you need and want from your medical care team. We are here to listen and help you know what choices you have. We want you to always be honest with yourself and us about your needs. Our goal is to gain your trust and help you find your sources of strength.
Your Gender-Affirming Care Plan
At Equitas Health, we believe our role is to help Trans and gender non-conforming people live truly. People in healthcare too often act as hurdles rather than facilitators. We use Informed Consent and Shared Decision Making when planning your care and prescribing hormones. This means that we will give you all the information you want and need, so you can make choices that are right for you.
Gender-Affirming Hormone Therapy
The care team understands Gender Dysphoria and supports patients as they affirm their identity. At Equitas Health, you do not need an okay from a psychiatrist or therapist to start gender-affirming hormone therapy. Your care team will listen to you and help you map out a hormone plan to reach your goals, including those that affirm non-binary or genderqueer identities. We know that no two paths to affirmation are the same.

Your personal needs and goals are important to us. Services include:

Medical Care
You’re in the driver’s seat. You know where you want to go. We are the navigators—ready to get you there as quickly and safely as possible.
Personalized Care
Does gender affirming care require hormone therapy?
Only if you want it to. Whatever your path, we’re here to affirm your identity and offer you choices.
Non-Binary Identities
I'm genderqueer. Is Gender Affirming Care for me?
Yes! We offer affirming services for people who identify outside of the gender binary. We are here for you.
Informed Consent
Do I need approval from a psychiatrist to start treatment?
No. Our providers will listen to you and help you map out a hormone regimen that will reach your goals.
Mental Health
Stigma causes many trans and non-binary people to experience depression and other mental illness. But it doesn’t have to keep you from care.
Counseling
How can mental health counseling help?
Our mental health counselors are here to affirm your identity and help you thrive. You are not alone!
Psychiatry
How do I know if I need psychiatry?
You'll start by meeting with a mental health counselor to determine if medication could be helpful to you.
Addiction Recovery
How can I tell if I have an addiction?
If substance use feels like your only way to cope or gets in the way of other needs, our recovery specialists can help.
Pharmacy
Our pharmacy staff work at Equitas Health because they want to provide quality, affordable, affirming care to LGBTQ+ people.
Knowledgeable
Will hormone therapy affect my other medications?
Our pharmacists are experts in gender affirming care. They can help you manage your medications safely.
Affirming
What if I have to give myself injections?
Our pharmacists will teach you to inject your medications safely and support you until you feel comfortable.
Convenient
What if I don’t live near Equitas Health?
Don’t worry! We offer free delivery to patients who live in Ohio. (Some restrictions apply.)
