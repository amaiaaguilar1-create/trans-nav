Our Founder
Built from lived experience.
Rooted in community.
In 2018, Iya Dammons founded Baltimore Safe Haven from her own lived experience navigating homelessness, housing instability, and a system that wasn't built for people like her. What started as a community effort became Maryland's only organization of its kind — a Black-led, community-rooted wellness center serving thousands of people across housing, health, and community services.
Today, BSH operates from 814 N. Collington Ave. in Baltimore, delivering person-centered care, housing placement, and wraparound support services to those who need them most.
"We lead by example, by lived experience — because we know exactly what our community needs."
Iya Dammons
Founder & Executive Director, Baltimore Safe Haven
What We Do
Whole-person services for every step of the journey.
From emergency housing placement to long-term health and wellness, BSH offers a full continuum of person-centered services — meeting people where they are, without barriers.
Housing Services
Emergency placement, Rapid Rehousing, Permanent Supportive Housing, and transitional housing — we help individuals and families achieve and maintain stable housing.

Health & Wellness
HIV prevention, PrEP linkage, mental health support, peer counseling, and connection to behavioral health resources — comprehensive care under one roof.
Learn more →
Community Support
Low-barrier, evidence-based support services, street outreach, drop-in center access, and connection to recovery and treatment resources for individuals facing complex barriers.

Legal & Advocacy
Legal navigation support, rights education, and advocacy resources — helping community members understand and access the protections and services available to them.
