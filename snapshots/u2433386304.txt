Need coverage now? You may be eligible for Special Enrollment.

Losing MaineCare?
If you recently lost MaineCare coverage and don't have access to health insurance from another source, you can likely find affordable coverage through CoverME.gov. Enroll within 90 days of your MaineCare loss through a "Recently Lost MaineCare" Special Enrollment Period. To enroll, contact our Consumer Assistance Center as soon as possible.
