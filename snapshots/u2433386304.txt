If you need to enroll in or change your 2026 coverage
Life changes like losing coverage, getting married, or moving to Maine may open a Special Enrollment Period for you. This will allow you to enroll in coverage outside of the Open Enrollment Period.
Additionally, if your household income or size change during the year, it is important to update your CoverME.gov application as soon as possible.
