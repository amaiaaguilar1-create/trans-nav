Our Programs & Services
The Center on Colfax provides a host of free services, targeted programs, enriching cultural events, and more!
General Services
HIV/STI screenings, fingerprinting services, insurance help, social support, peer support groups, fitness classes, LGBTQ+ lending library, and more.
Learn More
LGBTQ History Project
Collecting, preserving, archiving, and sharing LGBTQ+ history with the community through public exhibits and events, oral histories, and document archives.
Learn More
Range Education
Advancing equity in all spaces through Inclusion, Diversity, Equity, and Accessibility cultural humility workshops, consulting, workforce development, and partnerships.
Learn More
West of 50
An inclusive LGBTQ+ older adults space focused on creative aging, healthy living, and social connection through social, educational, and support programs and events.
Learn More
Rainbow Alley (Youth)
A safe, brave space for LGBTQ+ youth (ages 10-17) to find support and acceptance through special events, weekly drop-in times, workshops about life skills and wellness, and more.
Learn More
Young Adult Program
A supportive space for LGBTQ+ individuals ages 18-25 to engage with educational workshops, participate in community events, and build community supported independence and close relationships with peers.
Learn More
Glass Lawler Mental Health
Free culturally responsive mental health services for LGBTQ+ adults (18+), and peer support groups and community events hosted in a welcoming environment.
Learn More
Transgender Program
A welcoming, affirming community for transgender and gender diverse individuals, featuring social support groups, resources, and special events tailored to the community.
Learn More
