Legal Aid of Western Missouri
Justice for all
Legal Aid of Western Missouri doesn&#8217;t charge for legal services. We will never ask you to pay over the phone or online, and we will never request your bank account details or other sensitive banking information. If anyone claiming to be from Legal Aid asks for payment or personal banking information, it is a scam. Please contact us directly if you have any doubts or concerns.
Legal Aid of Western Missouri provides FREE civil (non-criminal) legal assistance to people who need it most and can afford it least. Every day, Missourians are denied the basic rights to which they are entitled under the law simply because they can’t afford an attorney. We’re on a mission to change that!
The availability of free civil legal aid can make all the difference to our neighbors who are fighting to stay in their homes, escape domestic violence, secure veterans’ benefits or address many other legal challenges that go to the heart of their security and well-being.
We serve 40 counties in Missouri where approximately 300,000 individuals and families experience poverty — most of whom you will never meet or even know. But they are a lot like you. They have families, hopes and dreams. They are your neighbors. They live in the communities you call home.
