We're so much more than birth control.
At Planned Parenthood of Northern New England, we're proud to offer a full range of reproductive and sexual health care services. Click on any topic to learn more.
Abortion services (procedural abortion or medication abortion)
Anti-anxiety medicine to increase comfort during IUD insertions/removals; implant insertions/removals; vasectomy; colposcopy; and biopsies.
Birth control (18 different methods including the pill, patch, shot, IUD, implant, vasectomy)
Cervical, breast , colorectal, and other cancer screenings
Emergency contraception (aka the "Morning After Pill") -- pills or IUD
Erectile dysfunction treatment
Follow up services for abnormal cervical cancer screenings and HPV
Gender affirming hormone therapy
HIV testing and prevention with PrEP and PEP
Help quitting smoking
Initial infertility work-up
Menopause and Perimenopause care
Miscarriage care
Patient support counseling (available at health centers in VT and Portland, ME)
Pelvic pain, bleeding, and other reproductive health concerns
Preconception education
Pregnancy Testing and a discussion of options
Reproductive life planning and goal setting
STD testing and treatment , and prevention services.
Vaccines for HPV , the Flu, Hepatitis and more
Vasectomies
Well-person checkups , a comprehensive, yearly visit, which includes some primary care services, and is personalized to fit you and your needs.
