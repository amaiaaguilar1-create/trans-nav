Bitcoin Depot Customers May Be Eligible for Refund
Bitcoin Depot Operating, LLC. (Bitcoin Depot) which operated cryptocurrency ATM kiosks, filed for bankruptcy last month and has ceased operations. Arizona consumers of Bitcoin Depot may be eligible for compensation for refunds.
See Press Release
Arizona Automobile Theft Authority Reports 25% Decrease in 2025 Vehicle Thefts
Data compiled by the Auto Crime Tracker database shows that in 2025, vehicle thefts totaled 12,686—a significant decrease from the 17,010 vehicles stolen in 2024. 2025 marks the third consecutive year Arizona has seen a decrease in vehicle thefts.
