Welcome to the Arlene Cooper Community Health Center!
401 S. Maryland Parkway
The Arlene Cooper Community Health Center recognizes sexual healthcare as part of one’s basic healthcare regimen. The ACCHC offers fast, free, and comprehensive testing for HIV. We offer sex-positive, safe, non-judgmental, supportive prevention and screening for sexually transmitted infections (STIs) to the community for a low cost. The Center empowers the people within our LGBTQIA+ community as well as surrounding neighborhoods with an awareness of their healthcare by enabling them to take control of their wellness.
Appointments are highly encouraged; however, walk-in services are available for a limited number of patients daily. Photo ID, military or student ID is required. An out of state ID can be accepted. Payment may be made by cash, debit, Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover and we are also able to bill certain insurance for services rendered.
To better serve our community, we are transitioning from online appointment scheduling to phone scheduling. Moving forward, please call us at 702-802-5432 to schedule your appointment.
This change allows our team to speak with you directly, better understand your needs, answer questions, and make sure you are scheduled for the appropriate service.
