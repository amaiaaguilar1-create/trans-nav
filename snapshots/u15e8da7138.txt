Locations
Greater Portland Health offers 17 convenient health center locations throughout Portland, South Portland, and Westbrook. To learn more or make an appointment, please call us at 207-874-2141 .
If you have a medical emergency, immediately call 911. If you require non-emergency medical attention during the times that we are closed, please call 207-874-2141 for our after-hours service.

654 Riverside Street
Greater Portland Health provides on-site healthcare services at the City of Portland's Homeless Services Center at 654 Riverside Street, a 24/7 emergency shelter for adults. Our team offers primary care, behavioral health services, and support connecting individuals to additional medical and community resources. Working alongside local partners, we help ensure people staying at the shelter have access to comprehensive care, while the center also provides meals, case management, and housing navigation support.

Mobile Medical Van
Greater Portland Health's mobile medical van delivers essential healthcare services directly to people in the community who may have difficulty accessing traditional care. Since launching in 2024, the van has provided medical care, and outreach support at locations such as community sites and partner organizations. By bringing care directly to patients, the mobile unit improves access, strengthens connections, and helps individuals stay engaged with their healthcare.

Federal Tort Claims Act
This health center receives HHS funding and has federal Public Health Service (PHS) deemed status with respect to certain health or health-related claims, including medical malpractice claims, for itself and its covered individuals. Greater Portland Health and its employees are covered by the Federal Tort Claims Act.
