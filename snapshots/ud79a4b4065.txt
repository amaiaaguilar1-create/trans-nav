Ramsey County Self-Help Service Center
The Ramsey County Self-Help Center offers help on Family Court issues, Criminal Expungements, Juvenile Expungements, Step-Parent Adoptions, Adult Adoptions, Civil Name Changes, and Guardianships/Conservatorships. The Center can help you find information and services about your legal problem if you are not represented by an attorney. Legal information includes court forms and tools to help you complete court forms, answers to general questions about court process or procedure, and help preparing for your day in court. The Center can also help review your completed court forms. Please see the Applications Process tab below for court form review information.
