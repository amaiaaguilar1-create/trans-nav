ID: Obtaining an Identification Card
Any person who can prove they are in the U.S. legally and show proof of a physical address in Maine may apply for a state non-driver identification card .
If your Social Security number is not on file with the Secretary of State, you must provide your Social Security number.
If you believe you are ineligible for a Social Security number, you must present an unexpired immigration document, such as an I-94, to help us determine your ineligibility.
All motor vehicle branch offices and mobile unit locations issue State Identification Cards. The fee is $5.
The fee for a REAL ID State Identification Card is $30. Please review requirements on the REAL ID page. The initial REAL ID license/ID card must be completed in person.
Identification cards will be mailed within two-to-three weeks from the date ordered.
Renewal of your identification card follows the same process as driver's license renewal , and is available through the online service or in-person at the BMV.
