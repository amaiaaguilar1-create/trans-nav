Required Documents
Documents Must Be
Original or certified copy with raised seal, stamp, or signature, and unexpired.
Documents Cannot Be
Photocopies, faxed copies, laminated birth certificates, or hospital certificates.
Documents Stored
All documents are imaged, stored in a secure database, and returned to you.
Document Approval
May take more than one day and require an additional visit.
Name Change
View the list of acceptable documents if your name has changed.
Translation
Foreign documents must be translated to English by a certified Translator .
