Services at our Maine Family Planning Gender Clinics include:
Hormonal transition therapy & monitoring for trans people 16 years old & over
Onsite self-injection lessons for hormone injections
Referrals to specialty providers & community resources, including mental, behavioral, & medical providers
Yearly wellness visits, preventive care, birth control & safer sex supplies, STD testing & treatment , and abortion for patients of any gender
Our Gender Clinics offer:
Patient-friendly hours
MaineCare, Medicare, and private insurance are accepted for covered services. VISA, MasterCard, debit cards, cash and personal checks are accepted for those paying out of pocket
For gender-affirming healthcare services, we do have a sliding fee scale based on household income for people without insurance
We can sign up income eligible uninsured or underinsured people for Medicaid limited family planning benefits, which cover most reproductive health services such as yearly wellness visits, STI testing, but do not cover specific gender-affirming healthcare services and medication
We will work with you and (if applicable) your insurance carrier to help make services and supplies as affordable as possible
We have providers in our Ellsworth, Farmington, Lewiston, Norway, Belfast, Thomaston, and Bangor health centers who provide gender-affirming healthcare services. Through our telehealth system, we usually can provide services to our patients from a site closer to home. Please call 207-922-3222 for more information and to schedule an appointment.
If you already have an appointment for an initial visit, it would save some time if you logged into your Patient Portal account and filled out the health history form before the appointment.
