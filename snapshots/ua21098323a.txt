Gender-Affirming Care
Transgender and gender nonconforming people have the same healthcare needs as cisgender people, such as basic physical exams, preventive care, and STI testing. But you may also have special healthcare concerns and needs. If you wish to transition medically by using hormones or having surgery, expert care is needed.
