Experience The Center.
For more than 40 years The Center has been at the core of New York's LGBTQ+ community. As a direct service provider, a leading LGBTQ+ advocate, and an affirming safe space, The Center stands as a vital pillar of support, activism, and community building for LGBTQ+ New Yorkers.

Resources & Services for Communities Under Attack
The Center is here for LGBTQ+ New Yorkers—always. When our communities are under attack, we provide safety, support, and resources to ensure no one in our community stands alone.

In 1986, I was a lonely 10-year-old brown butch kid in Little Rock, unsure why I should even exist. Growing up as the first-generation daughter of Catholic Filipino immigrants in Arkansas was tough. Fortunately, my journey brought me to New York and to The Center, where I felt, for the first time, the collective energy of a space meant to welcome me without judgment. Empowered by that acceptance, that kid from Arkansas is now a super gay, proud, silver-haired dyke on a bike, grateful to be part of such a resilient community. To the beautiful people of The Center, the basecamp for my chosen family, thank you for everything you do for us. You save lives.
Jen, Sirens Women's+ Motorcycle Club of NYC
