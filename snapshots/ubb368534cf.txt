Lawyer Referral Service (LRS)
The Guam Bar Association provides a complimentary lawyer referral service to help individuals find a private attorney or law firm that suits their legal needs. While we are not staffed with attorneys and cannot provide legal advice, we can assist in connecting you with a lawyer who can.
For more information or assistance, please contact GBA at info@guambar.org or 671-989-4227.
-------------------------
Individuals in need of legal assistance may also qualify for free or low-cost services through the following organizations:
Civil Law Center - Public Defender Service Corporation
Guam Legal Services Corporation - Disability Law Center
Micronesian Legal Services
View the Civil Indigent Legal Assistance Reference Guide for more information on each organization’s eligibility requirements, program restrictions, and available services.
