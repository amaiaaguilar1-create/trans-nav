Overview
The Office of Vital Statistics (OVS) is responsible for the registration, maintenance, processing, and issuance of official vital records for Guam residents. As part of the Department of Public Health and Social Services (DPHSS), the Office of Vital Statistics plays an important role in maintaining accurate and secure records involving births, deaths, marriages, and other official life events.
Vital records serve as important legal documents used for identification, citizenship verification, passports, school enrollment, employment, healthcare, insurance, benefits, and other official purposes. The Office of Vital Statistics works to ensure records are processed accurately, securely, and in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.
The office supports individuals, families, government agencies, healthcare providers, funeral homes, legal representatives, and other authorized parties requiring certified copies or official documentation.
Services Provided
The Office of Vital Statistics may provide services involving:
Birth certificates
Death certificates
Marriage certificates
Vital record verification
Certified copy requests
Record searches and processing
Amendments and corrections
Delayed registrations
Genealogical and historical record requests
Administrative processing and documentation support
Services and processing requirements may vary depending on the type of request and applicable regulations.
Birth Certificates
Birth certificates are official legal documents used for identification and verification purposes.
Certified birth certificates may be required for:
Passports
School enrollment
Employment
Healthcare services
Government benefits
Identification applications
Citizenship and immigration documentation
Insurance and legal matters
Applicants requesting birth certificates may be required to provide identification and supporting documentation according to established requirements.
Death Certificates
Death certificates are official records documenting a person’s death and may be required for legal, administrative, and family-related matters.
Death certificates may be needed for:
Insurance claims
Estate and probate matters
Funeral arrangements
Government benefit processing
Financial and legal documentation
Family records
The Office works with healthcare providers, funeral homes, and authorized representatives to process death records accurately and efficiently.
Marriage Certificates
Marriage certificates provide official legal documentation of marriages registered within Guam.
Certified marriage certificates may be used for:
Legal name changes
Immigration and citizenship matters
Insurance and benefit enrollment
Family and legal documentation
Government records and identification updates
Applicants requesting certified marriage records may be required to provide valid identification and supporting information.
Record Requests and Processing
Individuals requesting vital records may need to provide:
Government-issued identification
Application forms
Required fees
Supporting documentation
Proof of eligibility or authorization when applicable
Processing times may vary depending on request volume, record availability, and verification requirements.
The Office encourages residents to carefully review submission requirements before requesting records or services.
Amendments and Corrections
The Office of Vital Statistics may assist with certain record amendments or corrections involving:
Clerical errors
Name corrections
Delayed registrations
Supplemental documentation requests
Administrative record updates
Additional documentation, legal records, or supporting materials may be required depending on the nature of the request.
Security and Confidentiality
Vital records contain sensitive personal information and are protected according to applicable laws, regulations, and confidentiality requirements.
The Office of Vital Statistics works to ensure:
Secure handling of records
Protection of personal information
Verification of authorized requests
Accurate record maintenance
Compliance with legal requirements
Only authorized individuals or parties may be permitted to obtain certain records or certified copies.
Community and Government Support
The Office of Vital Statistics supports residents, families, healthcare providers, funeral homes, legal representatives, and government agencies by maintaining accurate public records and processing official documentation requests.
The office plays an important role in supporting public administration, legal documentation, healthcare coordination, and government recordkeeping throughout Guam.
Public Information and Assistance
Residents seeking additional information regarding vital records, application procedures, eligibility requirements, fees, or processing guidance are encouraged to contact the Office of Vital Statistics directly.
Public assistance may include:
Application guidance
Record request information
Identification requirements
Office procedures and processing updates
General service information
The Office of Vital Statistics remains committed to providing accurate, professional, and efficient service to Guam residents and authorized requestors.
Need a Birth, Death, or Marriage Certificate?
The Office of Vital Statistics assists eligible individuals and authorized parties with obtaining certified copies of official vital records and related documentation services.
For additional information regarding requests, office procedures, identification requirements, and processing guidelines, please contact the Office of Vital Statistics.
