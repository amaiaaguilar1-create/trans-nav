Mission
Our mission is to advance health equity and housing stability, with a focus on individuals and communities affected by HIV. We strive to create an inclusive society where everyone can live authentically, free from stigma and discrimination.
History
Since its founding as Charlotte’s first Ryan White HIV/AIDS Program administrator, CCP has remained a leader in advancing equitable health and housing outcomes. Our work reflects the resilience and strength of the communities we serve, with a steadfast commitment to inclusion, dignity, and justice.
Community
At CCP, we are a trusted partner to individuals from underserved communities—including those experiencing housing insecurity, poverty, discrimination, or limited access to services.
OUR INTRODUCTION
A Welcoming Space for All
Today, CCP is a trusted partner for people affected by HIV and members of LGB communities. We’ve created a space where:
Services are tailored to each person’s unique journey
Affirming environments foster connection and belonging
Everyone is supported in living as their authentic self
More Than Services &#8211; We Build Community
At CCP, we don’t just provide care, we nurture community. Your story shapes our mission. Your authenticity strengthens our collective impact.
Housing Support
CCP enhances housing stability for underserved communities, addressing the housing crisis with a focus on safety, health, and supportive care.
Health & Prevention
We provide HIV prevention, testing, and care linkage while offering mental health and substance misuse support to empower individuals to live healthy, authentic lives.
