Connecting All San Francisco Residents to Care
Healthy San Francisco offers low-cost health care to people in the city who don’t have insurance.
A Health Program For Our Community
Created in 2006 by the San Francisco Department of Public Health , Healthy San Francisco (HSF) helps uninsured San Francisco residents get low-cost, ongoing health care. It is an easy way to stay healthy and get care when you need it.
Read more about the program
Do I Qualify For HSF?
To qualify for HSF, you must meet these requirements:
You do not have health insurance
You live in San Francisco
You are 18 years or older

HSF participants can get basic health care like:
Primary and specialty care
Prescriptions and lab tests

Get More Benefits With Medi-Cal
Healthy San Francisco is not insurance. Insurance gives you more benefits. If you can, getting health insurance like Medi-Cal is the best choice. Medi-Cal offers free or low-cost health care for people with low income.
The SFHP Service Center can see if you qualify and help you enroll in Medi-Cal and other health programs.

Is It Time to Renew HSF?
You must reapply for Healthy San Francisco every 12 months. This helps you keep your access to care.
