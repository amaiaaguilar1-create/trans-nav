Transgender Health Program
Affirming, Compassionate Care for Transgender and Gender-Diverse Communities
St. John’s Community Health is committed to protective and transparent healthcare regardless of gender identity, sexual orientation, and/or presentation. We provide comprehensive transgender health services in a safe and welcoming environment at our clinics where you are free to be yourself.
Find a Clinic & Get Care
Transgender Health Services Offered
• Primary and preventive care – medical, dental, behavioral health and pharmacy services
• Hormone replacement therapy
• Referrals for gender confirming surgeries
• Referrals to transgender advocates, legal support, and other services
• Name and/or gender marker changes
• Behavioral and mental health care – individual counseling, family counseling, and support groups
• HIV and STI testing, counseling, and treatment
• Health insurance enrollment
• Assistance with legal name/gender change paperwork
• PEP and PrEP
Become a New Patient
Getting started with the Transgender Health Program is simple. Whether you're seeking gender-affirming care, primary services, or mental health support, we’re here to guide you every step of the way.
Here’s how to begin:
Step 1: Contact Us
Call or visit one of our clinic locations to speak with our team and schedule your first appointment.
Step 2: Complete Initial Intake Form & Review New Patient Packet
To save time, you can complete your intake form online—just click the “Initial Intake Form” button on this page.
Step 3: Meet Your Care Team
At your first visit, you'll meet with a provider who listens, affirms your identity, and works with you to create a personalized care plan.

Insurance and Payment Information
At St. John’s, anyone is eligible to receive services, no matter their ability to pay. We accept Medi-Cal, Medicare and many other insurance plans. We have benefits counselors at all of our health centers to help patients determine their eligibility for insurance programs. For uninsured patients we offer services on a sliding scale cash basis.

Pharmacy, Prescription and Laboratory Services
We offer Pharmacy, prescription and Laboratory services including: Senior programs, Public-Private Partnership, Sliding Fee Scale Pharmacy Programs, diagnostic and laboratory procedures, HIV and STD testing and more. Our Dispensaries offer prescription services to enrolled clinic patients.
