Support The Q Center
Q Center is Portland's LGBTQIA2S+ community center, a place to find connection, support, and belonging. We bring people together through year-round programs, groups, and events, while helping community members navigate resources when life gets complicated. In a world that can feel increasingly hostile, we're committed to being a steady, affirming space where our community is seen, protected, and empowered to thrive.
Your Support
Keep Q Center here for our community. Community is built, and protected, together. Your donation helps provide affirming programs, resources, and connection for LGBTQIA2S+ people in Portland, especially when it's needed most.

Marsha P. Johnson Fund
Q Center's Marsha P. Johnson Community Fund, launched in 2019, provides emergency microgrants to Black and Indigenous transgender people in honor of Marsha P. Johnson's legacy of community care and liberation. Created in response to persistent systemic inequities

Standing On Business Partners
Q Center's Standing on Business Program is a community-rooted partnership initiative that invites local businesses, entrepreneurs, civic leaders, and values-aligned supporters to invest in queer and trans economic wellbeing across Oregon. Q Programs
Q Center's programs are designed to support, connect, and empower LGBTQIA2S+ people at every stage of life. From peer-led support groups and community gatherings to educational workshops, creative opportunities, and leadership development, our programs foster belonging, resilience, and meaningful connection.
​
Whether you're looking for support, new skills, or a place to build community, there's a program for you.
