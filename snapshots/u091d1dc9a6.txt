Mission
CGC partners with CNMI individuals, families, and communities towards a life of hope, healing, and
health
through substance abuse prevention and the promotion of wellness and recovery.
Vision
We envision that all CNMI residents fully integrate behavioral health and wellness in safe, resilient,
drug-free homes and communities.

Mental Health Services
Provides counseling, therapy, and psychiatric care to support individuals experiencing mental health
challenges such as anxiety, depression, or trauma.
Addictions Services
Offers treatment and recovery support for individuals struggling with substance use, including
counseling, detox services, and relapse prevention.
Community Outreach & Response Services
Delivers mental health support in the community, including crisis response, education, and outreach to
individuals and families in need.
Adult Mental Health
Mental health is a topic that touches the lives of countless individuals, and our program is designed
to cater to a wide range of needs.
Community Support & Training
In today's rapidly evolving world, the need for community support and training has never been greater.
Disaster Recovery
Our Disaster Recovery Program is designed to offer comprehensive assistance, including
guidance on disaster preparedness, access to emergency relief, and resources for recovery and
resilience.
Psychiatry
Mental health is an essential aspect of our overall well-being, influencing how we think, feel, and
interact with the world.
Substance Abuse Treatment & Recovery Clinic
Our Substance Abuse Treatment & Recovery Clinic Program is designed to offer comprehensive care,
from assessment and detoxification to counseling, therapy, and long-term support.
Suicide Prevention
Our Suicide Prevention Program is committed to raising awareness, offering crisis intervention, and
providing a comprehensive range of resources to prevent suicide and promote mental well-being.
Youth Mental Health
Our Youth Mental Health Program is designed to offer comprehensive
care, including counseling, therapy, education, and resources, to address these challenges and empower
young individuals to lead healthy, fulfilling lives.
