Application for Change of Name of an Adult with No Minor Children
You may use the forms on this page if the following factors apply to your situation :
You are a resident of the county where you are filing this request, AND ,
You want to ask the Court to change your name, AND ,
You are an adult 18 years or older, AND ,
You do not have a minor child(ren), AND ,
You are prepared under penalty of perjury to inform the Court whether you have ever been convicted of a felony and whether there are any pending charges against you for a felony or other offense involving false statements or misrepresentation of identity, AND ,
You understand the name change will neither harm your rights nor release you from any obligations or liabilities incurred under your current name, AND ,
You are not changing your name to that of another person for the purpose of committing any crime or furthering any offense involving fraud or misrepresentation of identity.
DO NOT USE THESE FORMS TO CHANGE YOUR NAME BECAUSE YOU GOT MARRIED. You do not need to request a name change through the Court if you get married and want to use your spouse's last name.
Packets contain ALL forms and ALL instructions needed for this process.
