Featured Case
March 11 ICE Raid FTCA Claims
We submitted legal claims under the Federal Torts Claim Act (FTCA) on behalf of Johana Patin Patin and José Estrada Jerez, whose rights were violated by ICE when agents raided their home in search of a man they did not know and who was not there.
These complaints are part of a coordinated effort to hold the federal government accountable for its lawless immigration enforcement campaign, featuring 54 complaints filed by 17 ACLU affiliates and their partners.
