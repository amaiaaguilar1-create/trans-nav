Additional Fee Information for Examiners
1. The first time an applicant takes an exam they are charged $3.00. Please click here for more information on
testing fees.
2. Reexams within a 6-month period are $1.50.
3. If an applicant takes their reexam after 6 months from the date of their last exam, they will be charged the $3.00
abandonment fee, and will not be charged the $1.50.
4. A testing fee is not charged for those who use a driver’s education form.
5. Once an applicant pays their license fee, that is paid until they pass their exam. For example, if an applicant came
in and paid their license and testing fee in 2018 but failed their exam and are now back in 2021 with a driver’s
education completion form, they wouldn’t have to pay anything.
6. Those testing for haz-mat on a haz-mat renewal do not pay the $3.00 for their first attempt that the exam. They do
get charged the $1.50 for any reexams though. Please click here for more information on haz-mat renewals.
7. If an applicant does not receive their credential in the mail and there is no mistake on our side, the applicant is
charged the full replacement fee. Please click here for more information.
8. If an applicant renews their license between 1 year to 1 day after they expire, they are charged the $1.00 penalty
fee. Click here for more information on the penalty fee.
9. If an applicant is obtaining a Kansas license after living in the state for over 150 days, they are charged the $1.00
penalty fee only on a license issuance.
10. Applicants with a DCF voucher are not charged in the office, but the fees are not set to 0 when processing. Please
click here for more information.
11. Applicants may obtain an ID at no fee if it is needed to vote. Please click here for more information.
12. Replacements are $16.00 for a license or permit and $12.00 for an ID and are when the applicant does not have a
current credential to show, or the current credential is destroyed to where it is no longer legible. An applicant does
not get charged extra for motorcycle if it was already on the current credential, the M fee must be deleted for
changes and replacements. Please see KSA 8-246.
13. Name changes are also charged the replacement fees. So, a name change on a license or permit is $16.00 and a
name change on an ID is $12.00.
14. Changes are $8.00. These are when someone has a credential to present, but needs a change made, like a new
photo, adding REAL ID, having a restriction removed, changing address, etc.….
15. Applicants should never be charged to correct an examiner error. For example, an applicant did not get their
license in the mail because an examiner made a typo in the address.
