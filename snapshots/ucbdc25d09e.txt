About the Resource Center
Opened on December 1, 2023, the Resource Center was created to serve as a true one-stop shop for all four offices of the Circuit Court Clerk. It provides in-person, hands-on support for residents seeking more in-depth assistance with navigating court services. Guided by the Circuit Court Clerk’s Four Pillars—Service, Integrity, Experience, and Innovation—the Resource Center team is dedicated to delivering an exceptional customer experience. Visitors have access to court information, self-help tools, and resources from partnering Metro agencies and offices, ensuring every person who walks in receives the support they need.
Who is this page for?
Self-represented litigants. Anyone who needs the right forms, payments, and lookups fast.
Where is the Resource Center?
1 Public Square Ste 303. Hours 8:00 to 4:30. Phone (615) 862-5207.
How can I pay?
Cash, major cards, and Tap to Pay (Apple Pay / Google Pay) accepted.
A 2.25% card service fee applies. No refunds.
Interpreters and ADA
ILA translator available. ADA contact: Trey Collier, (615) 880-3309.
Can staff give legal advice?
No. Read the instructions on the forms or contact the Resource Center for general guidance. For advice, talk to a lawyer.

Who qualifies for Irreconcilable Differences divorce?
Both parties agree. Cases with or without minor children are allowed. At least one party is a Davidson County resident or the parties separated in Davidson County.
What are the fees?
$184.50 without minor children. $259.50 with minor children. Notary $2.50. Copies $0.50 per page.
What ID do I need for notary service?
Bring a government issued photo ID.
How long does it take?
No children: 60 days before you can file a Motion to Set Final Hearing. With children: 90 days. The Marital Dissolution Agreement expires after 180 days.
Where are the forms?
Use the official Circuit divorce forms on our site. See Circuit Forms in the links above.

What is a small estate?
Assets valued at $50,000 or less. Use the small estate process if it fits your situation.
Which small estate form do I use?
With a will: Small Estate Petition with Will .
Without a will: Small Estate Petition without Will .
Do I need a hearing for a name change?
Yes. Use this form: Petition for Adult Legal Name Change . A hearing is required.

What do I need to pay a ticket?
Ticket number or your name and date of birth. Card payments may include a service fee.
Can I get a court date?
Some citations allow scheduling a court date. Check your ticket or contact the Resource Center (615) 862-5207 .
Lost the ticket?
Search the docket or contact the Resource Center (615) 862-5207 .
