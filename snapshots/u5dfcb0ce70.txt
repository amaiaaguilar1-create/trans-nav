Instructions: 1) An individual 18 years of age or older may use this form to petition for a change of his or her name.
2) The petition must be filed in the court for the probate district in which the petitioner resides and
accompanied by (a) PC-910, Affidavit RE Change of Name, (b) a certified copy of the petitioner’s long
form birth certificate, (c) two forms of identification, one of which must be photographic identification,
and (d) Confidential Sheet, PC-901CI with the petitioner’s confidential social security number.
3) The court will conduct a search of the sex offender and deadly weapon offender registry and may
conduct a full criminal background check.
4) For more information, see Probate Court Rules of Procedure sections 47.1 and 47.4.
5) Type or print in ink. Use an additional sheet, or PC-180, if more space is needed.
