Diversity Family Health - Oklahoma City
Oklahoma City is the corporate office for all Diversity Family Health locations. Opening in March of 2018, the current clinic in Shartel Towers has multiple providers addressing the unique needs of the LGBTQ community and has nurse practitioners that specialize in the area of Women's Health in addition to the core services. This location also has a number of vetted sub-specialists when our patients need the additional assistance from providers specializing in things like cardiology, rheumatology, or neurology. This clinic is open Monday through Friday from 9am to 5pm.

Russell Rooms, DNP, APRN, FNP-BC
Family Nurse Practitioner
Founder and Nurse Practitioner
Russell Rooms is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner with over 30 years in the medical field and a Nurse Practitioner since 2004. He has a Doctoral degree from Oklahoma City University in Nursing Practice with a concentration in Community and Public Health. Dr. Rooms specializes in the identification and treatment of HPV related anal cancers in men who have sex with men.
Russell created Diversity Family Health out of the need for the LGBTQ+ community in Oklahoma City to be able to receive competent culturally centered care in an environment free of discrimination and bias. Starting in a one exam room location in the Gayborhood in Oklahoma City, Diversity Family Health has now grown to have multiple locations in the state of Oklahoma and Arkansas.

Dr. Ralph Cornelius
Provider & Director of Strategy &
Marketing
Ralph Cornelius, DNP, APRN, FNP-C is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner at Diversity Family Health with a strong focus on equitable, affirming primary care for LGBTQ+ patients and people living with or at risk for HIV. He earned his Doctor of Nursing Practice from Northwestern Oklahoma State University, where his studies centered on healthcare equity and PrEP medication adherence.
Ralph completed a DNP project titled Injectable PrEP Gives Providers Another Option to Combat Oral PrEP Compliance, which he presented at the 2024 National LGBTQ Health Conference. His clinical interests include HIV treatment and prevention, PrEP, gender-affirming care, sexual health, and chronic disease management. He is particularly passionate about reducing barriers to care through patient-centered education and shared decision-making.
In addition to his clinical work, Ralph serves as Director of Marketing and Strategy at Diversity Family Health, where he supports outreach, education, and growth initiatives that expand access to affirming care. His background in communications and public service informs a practical, patient-centered approach to health education, community engagement, and advocacy.

Dr. Russell Rooms
Provider & Founder/CEO
Russell Rooms, DNP, APRN, FNP-BC , is a board certified Family Nurse Practitioner with over 30 years in the medical field and a Nurse Practitioner since 2004. He has a Master's degree from the University of Texas as an Emergency Clinical Nurse Specialist and a Post Masters degree as an Emergency Nurse Practitioner. Most recently Russell completed a Doctorate of Nursing Practice with a concentration in community and public health at Oklahoma City University where he focused on the prevention and treatment of anal cancers in men who have sex with men.
Russell specializes in conditions that effect the LGBTQ+ community with experience and training in gender affirming care, and HIV prevention and treatment. Russell also specializes in HPV related anal neoplasia and trained in high resolution anoscopy.
Additionally, Russell is a champion for human rights and an expert in sexual violence in the queer community. As a member of the American Academy of Forensic Sciences he works with other disciplines to ensure that sexual orientation and gender identity are included in the investigation of trauma and human rights violations.
