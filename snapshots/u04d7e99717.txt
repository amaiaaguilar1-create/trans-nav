Help! My Insurance Company Won’t Approve my Referral from Chase Brexton
If a Chase Brexton provider orders a referral to a specialist, sometimes an insurance company won’t approve the referral. This happens because your insurance company has a different doctor listed as your Primary Care Provider (PCP) than the Chase Brexton provider who ordered your referral. Insurance companies will only approve referrals from the PCP officially on file with them.
Because of this mismatch, your referral cannot be used yet at the specialist's office.
What You Need to Do (3 Steps)
Step 1: Call Your Insurance Company
Use the phone number on the back of your insurance card.
Ask them to update your Primary Care Provider (PCP) to your Chase Brexton provider.
Ask how long the change will take to process.
Step 2: Call Chase Brexton Referral Team
Once your insurance confirms the PCP change is complete, call:410-837-2050, ext.8857.
Let them know your PCP has been updated.
They will finalize and reissue your referral.
Step 3: Wait for the New Referral
Do not go to the specialist yet— the current referral won't be accepted.
Once Chase Brexton reissues the referral, you can schedule your specialist appointment.
Questions?
Call the Chase Brexton Clinical Referral Coordinator: 410-837-2050, ext. 8857.
