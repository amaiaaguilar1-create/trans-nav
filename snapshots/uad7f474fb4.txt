Jurisdictional Limits of Court
• $4,501 up to and including $50,000 - Concurrent jurisdiction with the General
District Court.
• More than $50,000 - Circuit Court has sole jurisdiction.
Civil Filing Fee Breakdown
Civil fees are assessed in accordance with specific statutes. Some fees apply only to
certain types of filings.
• Clerk’s Fees
Range from $1.50 to $290.00, as determined by statute and, in some cases,
based on the amount claimed.
• Writ Tax Fees
Range from $5.00 to $25.00, as determined by statute and based on the amount
claimed.

CCR-A-20 Civil Filing Fees and Instructions 2 July 2026
CASE TYPES
ADOPTION
(Please refer to Adoption Procedures Brochure)
Adoption petitions require:
• One (1) Adoption Case Cover Sheet
• Original notarized petition plus two (2) additional copies
• Endorsed Proposed Final Order for the Judge’s signature
• Completed VS-21 (Report of Adoption) – must be completed in black ink
• Completed VS-6 (Certification of Vital Record, if applicable) – must be
completed in black ink
• Any evidence, exhibits, or documentation in support of the complaint, plus two
(2) copies of each paper/document filed
COMPLAINT
• Original plus additional copies of Complaint for each service, if applicable
• One (1) Civil Case Cover Sheet
DEBTOR INTERROGATORIES
(Please refer to Debtor Interrogatories Guidelines)
Submit original plus two (2) copies of the following:
• Interrogatory Summons Form
• Affidavit
• List of Documents (if using form for Production of Documents)
• Original Exemption Claim Form
DIVORCE
(Please refer to the Pro Se Divorce Brochure)
• Original Complaint plus one (1) additional copy for service
• One (1) Divorce Cover Sheet
• Completed VS4 (Report of Divorce or Annulment) – must be completed in black
ink
• Additional recording fee is required for parties resuming their maiden name
GARNISHMENT
(Please refer to the Garnishment Instructions)
• Original Suggestion in Garnishment form (complete fully)
• Original Garnishment Summons form plus six (6) copies (complete fully)
• Original Garnishment Exemption Claim form plus six (6) copies (complete fully)
• Envelope addressed to Debtor
LEVY
(Please refer to Guidelines for Preparing Levy Requests)
• Original Levy Request plus five (5) additional copies
• Original Request for Exemption Claim form plus five (5) additional copies
