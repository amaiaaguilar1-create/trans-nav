Policy 22.01 Gender Change / Reassignment
Section: 1. Issuance of Driver’s License
Effective Date: 07/01/1991
Revised Date: 03/12/2009
Authority:
Administrative
Definition: If an applicant for or a holder of a Louisiana driver's license/identification
card indicates that he has undergone a gender change/reassignment procedure and
desires to change the gender identification on his driver's license/ID card, the change
will be noted and a new driver's license/ID card will be issued.
Requirements:
• A medical statement signed by a physician stating that the applicant has
undergone a successful gender change/reassignment.
• In addition, should the applicant or holder of a Louisiana driver's license/ID card
seek a name change due to the gender change/reassignment, a
certified or true copy of a court order must be presented.
