Vital Records Fees
Vital records are released only as certified or informational copies, per ARSD 44:09:06:02 . Find information on the fees for obtaining certified or informational copies of vital records in South Dakota, including birth, death, marriage, and divorce certificates. Also access information on fee waivers for military, Head Start, school enrollment, and baseball. See applicable fees below.
