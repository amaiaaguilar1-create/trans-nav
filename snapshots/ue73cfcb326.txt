Hours and locations
The general CrescentCare hours of operation are Monday through Friday 8:30 am to 5 pm. At this time, we are not holding in-person Saturday hours for any services.
If you have a non-life threatening urgent medical situation and the clinic is closed, please call our main number at 504-821-2601 and follow the prompts (press 3) to reach the after-hours answering service.
