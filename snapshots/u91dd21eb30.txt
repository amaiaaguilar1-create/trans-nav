A Word from Our CEO...
“Healthcare should be affirming, accessible, and rooted in dignity for every person. At Corktown Health, we are committed to building a model of care where LGBTQ+ individuals and underserved communities feel respected, valued, and empowered to thrive. We believe health equity requires more than treatment alone—it requires removing barriers, addressing the whole person, and creating spaces where people feel safe being themselves. Through compassionate, evidence-based care and strong community partnerships, we are working every day to advance a healthier, more inclusive future for all.”
Anthony Williams,
CEO of Corktown Health
