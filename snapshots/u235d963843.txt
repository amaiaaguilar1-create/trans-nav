Our Mission & The Importance of Civil Legal Aid
Community Legal Services is a non-profit law firm committed to increasing fairness in the civil justice system by advocating, litigating, and educating on behalf of Arizona’s most underserved communities. Civil legal aid helps ensure fairness for all in the justice system, regardless of how much money you have. It provides access to legal help for people to protect their livelihoods, their health, and their families.
Access to Justice for All
Civil legal aid connects Americans with a range of services including legal assistance and representation, self-help centers and other court-based services, free legal clinics and pro-bono assistance, and access to web-based information and forms that help guide them through complicated legal proceedings. CLS works to make our services accessible to all. In addition to making our services available to people with disabilities, we also provide translators for all languages.
Consumer Law
CLS helps people protect their consumer rights regarding purchases, income, property, contracts, loans and debts.
Domestic Violence & Family Law
CLS supports the safety and wellbeing of the family through the legal process, focusing on victims of domestic violence and children at risk.
Employment Law
CLS helps ensure safe job conditions, unemployment insurance, wages earned and freedom from discrimination.
Farmworker Program
CLS is dedicated to ensuring legal access and protecting the rights of agricultural workers throughout Arizona.
Public Benefits Unit
​CLS helps people obtain and preserve necessities of life including AHCCCS Healthcare coverage, Social Security disability benefits, Nutrition Assistance and Cash Assistance.
Housing Law
Whether you rent or own, CLS helps with healthy and safe living conditions and preserving housing.
Set Asides
You may qualify to set aside your conviction and restore your civil rights.
Tax Program
Through a grant from the IRS, our Tax Program helps clients who have IRS controversies.

Take a peek into Community Legal Services Prescott Office, where we serve our Yavapai county community!
