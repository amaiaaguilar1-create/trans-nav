Explore highlights of the work we do to ensure the safety and future of Trans people in Arkansas.
OUR WORK
Anti-Violence
Providing advocacy support for survivors of domestic & sexual violence, support in securing emergency housing & groceries, HIV & harm reduction support. And organizing and advocating for Trans people facing state-sponsored violence.
Education
Holding educational spaces in which people can grow and be challenged, and are expected to strive to be their best whole selves. We hold monthly community led skill-sharing workshops.
Immigration
Supporting Trans immigrants through legal advocacy, interpretation, translations and organizing, mobilizing and advocating for the abolishment of all cages.
Arkansas GSA Network Contact
Supporting and building the leadership of Trans and LGB+ youth in Arkansas. We are the Arkansas contact for GSA Newtork, and can help support youth who want to organize GSAs in their schools, colleges, or universities.
Community Care
Creating spaces in which Trans people can overcome feelings of isolation and individualism. Spaces in which we can provide collective community care and sustainability of ourselves and our movement.
Financial Support Funds
Supporting Trans people in Arkansas facing emergency situations and/or financial hardship.
