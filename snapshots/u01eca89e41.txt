Apply for Medicaid
You may apply in person, by mail, or online. When we receive your application, the Department of Workforce Services (DWS) will contact you by mail or phone. DWS may need more information to determine if you are eligible for a medical program. DWS will also tell you what proofs you need to provide, like a birth certificate.
