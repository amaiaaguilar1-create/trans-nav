Waikiki Health&#8217;s patient-centered HIV Early Intervention Services (HEIS) program is committed to the early detection and treatment of HIV, which can help prevent the onset of AIDS and other opportunistic infections. We provide a safe environment, welcoming everyone affected by or infected with HIV/AIDS.
Waikiki Health no longer has board-certified Infectious Disease (ID) providers. This change is reflected in our updated HRSA Form 5A. However, our experienced providers continue to offer high-quality care for patients living with HIV and Viral Hepatitis.
HIV Early Intervention Services include:
Free, confidential testing
Prevention and treatment
Health education
Medication counseling
Nutrition counseling
HEIS Outreach Program
Call (808) 922-4787 for an appointment.
HIV Early Intervention Services are available at these locations:
Ohua Clinic (Waikiki)
Makahiki Clinic (McCully-Moiliili)
HEIS Outreach Services are possible due to funding from the Department of Health, Alcohol and Drug Abuse Division.
