OUR SERVICES
Welcome to First Choice Community Healthcare. We are a recognized PCMH Joint Commission accredited healthcare facility.
Patients who receive care at First Choice Community Healthcare benefit from a team of providers who work together to create a personalized care plan for each patient, which helps to ensure that patients receive the right care at the right time. We provide patients with education and support to help them manage their health conditions, which can help to improve patient outcomes and reduce the risk of complications. By being a PCMH accredited facility, this helps reduce costs by providing preventive care and managing chronic conditions more effectively.
