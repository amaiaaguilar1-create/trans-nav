Rainy Day Fund
Columbia South Carolina
Mar 12
Written By Harriet Hancock LGBT Center
Rainy Day Fund seeks to increase access to justice by providing financial support and coordinating legal services for those whose main barrier to the legal system is financial. We accept referrals from case managers, service providers, social workers, and attorneys to ensure that we are supporting holistic representation.
One of the larger projects of RDF is to partner with the Harriet Hancock Center on name and gender marker change petitions by providing free fingerprinting services on the third Friday of each month from 1-4 PM at the HHC.
To get in touch with us, fill out the contact form on our website at rainydayfund.us .
