Birth Control
WV Health Right offers a range of birth control options free of charge to individuals ages 12 and older. Walk-ins for insertion of Nexplanon (a contraceptive implanted in the upper arm) are accepted daily, and an OB/GYN specialist is on-site once every month to provide IUDs by referral. We encourage patients to call ahead whenever possible.
Family Planning is available at our East End Clinic.
