Change a Birth Certificate
Use the forms and information on this page to request correction(s) of incorrect or missing information on a birth certificate.
Request changes
Request corrections to any incorrect or missing information on your birth certificate by downloading and completing the English Correction Request Form-Birth and the Spanish Correction Request Form-Birth. Detailed instructions are included with the form. Send the completed form along with any fees to:
Idaho Bureau of Vital Records and Health Statistics
PO Box 83720
Boise, ID 83720-0036
If any information is changing because of adoption, surrogacy, court-ordered name change, or if paternity has been determined, see the instructions and frequently asked questions below.
