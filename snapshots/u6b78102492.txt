What is National Coming Out Day?
National Coming Out Day is observed every year on October 11. It is a day that celebrates LGBTQ+ people who choose to live openly, and a day that reminds everyone how powerful it is to be known for who you are. Where it came from The day grew out of the National March on Washington for Lesbian and Gay Rights on October 11, 1987.
