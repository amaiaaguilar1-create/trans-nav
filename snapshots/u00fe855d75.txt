Justice, Community, Care:
Compassionate, free legal help for low-income Coloradans, seniors, and veterans
We know that civil legal representation can help keep Coloradans housed, fed, and safe. Colorado Legal Services is a nonprofit that offers free legal help on issues that are not criminal to low-income Coloradans who qualify.
We are committed to providing compassionate legal advocacy for Coloradans in some of life’s toughest moments.

Colorado Legal Services Helps Thousands of Coloradans Each Year
Colorado Legal Services is a non-profit agency that provides free, high-quality civil legal services to eligible low-income Coloradans and seniors.
CLS is led by a board of directors that includes attorneys, people eligible to be represented by CLS, and members of organizations that also serve low-income Coloradans.

Who Qualifies for Help?
You may qualify for free civil legal aid if you meet certain income and other requirements based on federal poverty guidelines, or if you are 60 years old or older. Please reach out or apply for help to learn more.

Want to Get Involved?
Are you looking to get involved with Colorado Legal Services and make an impact on Coloradans? There are three easy ways to get involved.
Volunteer
Colorado Legal Services has many volunteers and interns who contribute a wide range of knowledge and experience. Many are retirees, students, attorneys, or others who have an interest in law or are simply committed to helping in the community.
Volunteer With us
Give
The Legal Aid Foundation of Colorado raises money for Colorado Legal Services, the only nonprofit in the state that provides free legal services in civil cases to clients who could not otherwise afford them in every Colorado county.
For every client served by CLS, at least one income-eligible person is turned away. With your support, CLS is working to meet a constantly expanding need and help ensure justice for all is more than just a promise.
