How to Pay the Filing Fee
If you're filing something that requires a filing fee, you must pay the fee or your case may be delayed or dismissed.
These are ways you can pay the filing fee:
You can pay using a check or money order made out to Vermont Superior Court .
You can pay by credit card . There is a 2.39% convenience fee to pay by credit card. If you are filing by email, include your phone number in the body of your message and say you want to make your payment over the phone. The court will contact you to take your payment.
If you are filing in person you can pay with cash . Do not mail cash.
There is no filing fee for these cases:
Request for Relief from Abuse
Request for Abuse Prevention for a Vulnerable Adult
Request for Order Against Stalking
Request for Order Against Sexual Assault
Request for Extreme Risk Protection Order
Abusive Litigation
Request to Expunge or Seal a Criminal Record (Exception: there is a fee to ask to seal a conviction for driving under the influence of alcohol ( 23 V.S.A. § 1201(a) ) committed by someone when they were 25 or older).
Application to Waive Filing Fees and Service Costs
Most initial court filings and requests for relief require a filing fee. The court has an Application to Waive Filing Fees and Service Costs, sometimes called an Application to Proceed In Forma Pauperis, that you can file if you feel you cannot afford the fees.
