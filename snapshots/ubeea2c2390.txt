AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION 63-1-35, MISSISSIPPI CODE OF 1972, TO1 PROVIDE THAT THE COMMISSIONER MAY ONLY PRESCRIBE THE FORM OF2 LICENSE ISSUED UNDER THIS ARTICLE TO LICENSEES WHO ARE NOT UNITED3 STATES CITIZENS OR WHO DO NOT POSSESS A SOCIAL SECURITY NUMBER IF4 THE LICENSEE IS LAWFULLY PRESENT IN THE UNITED STATES; TO PROVIDE5 THAT SUCH A LICENSE SHALL INCLUDE A DESIGNATION THAT THE PERSON IS6 NOT A UNITED STATES CITIZEN AND THAT THE LICENSE SHALL EXPIRE ON7 THE DATE THAT THE PERSON IS NO LONGER LAWFULLY PRESENT IN THE8 UNITED STATES; TO REQUIRE ANY NEW, RENEWAL OR DUPLICATE DRIVER'S9
LICENSE, TEMPORARY DRIVING PERMIT OR COMMERCIAL DRIVER'S LICENSE10 ISSUED TO A PERSON UNDER THIS CHAPTER TO BEAR A DESIGNATION OF THE11 SEX OF THE INDIVIDUAL WHICH SHALL BE CONSISTENT WITH THE SEX OF12 THE INDIVIDUAL DETERMINED AT BIRTH; TO PROVIDE THAT A DRIVER'S13 LICENSE, OR OTHER PERMIT PURPORTING TO AUTHORIZE THE HOLDER TO14 OPERATE A MOTOR VEHICLE ON PUBLIC ROADWAYS, ISSUED BY ANOTHER15 STATE OR JURISDICTION TO A PERSON UNABLE TO PROVE LAWFUL PRESENCE16 IN THE UNITED STATES WHEN THE LICENSE OR PERMIT IS ISSUED, SHALL17 BE INVALID IN THIS STATE AND DOES NOT AUTHORIZE THE HOLDER TO18 OPERATE A MOTOR VEHICLE IN THIS STATE; TO REQUIRE A PEACE OFFICER19 WHO STOPS A PERSON DRIVING OR USING SUCH AN INVALID LICENSE TO20 ISSUE A CITATION TO THE DRIVER AND REFER THE PERSON TO THE UNITED21 STATES IMMIGRATION AND CUSTOMS ENFORCEMENT AGENCY; TO REQUIRE A22 JUSTICE COURT JUDGE, LICENSE EXAMINER, OR ANY AUTHORIZED EMPLOYEE23
OF THE COMMISSIONER WHO ENCOUNTERS A PERSON USING SUCH AN INVALID24 LICENSE TO REFER THE PERSON TO THE UNITED STATES IMMIGRATION AND25 CUSTOMS ENFORCEMENT AGENCY; TO REQUIRE THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC26 SAFETY TO ADOPT RULES AND REGULATIONS TO IMPLEMENT THIS SECTION;27 TO PROVIDE FOR SEVERABILITY; AND FOR RELATED PURPOSES.28
