Services
MBK's organizational infrastructure is built around three complementary Centers, each designed to support a coordinated approach to healthcare&mdash;focusing on prevention, treatment, and ongoing care across the communities we serve.
The Center for Community Based Programs
(CCBP)
The Community-Campus Building Partnership (CCBP) works to promote healthy lifestyles and build stronger communities by connecting individuals, local organizations, academic institutions, and public leaders. Through collaboration and shared resources, CCBP supports efforts to improve health outcomes and reduce the impact of preventable diseases.
Learn More
The Center for Research, Evaluation, and Environmental & Policy Change
(CREEP)
CREEP was created to achieve health equity among underserved populations through research, evaluation, and environmental and policy systems change initiatives that lead to new scientific knowledge, and provide information on program effectiveness, and evaluate data-driven systems change and intervention strategies.
Learn More
Open Arms
Healthcare Center
Open Arms Healthcare Center provides innovative, whole-person primary care services&mdash;including preventive care, clinical treatment, behavioral health support, PrEP services, and family planning&mdash;for individuals and families across Mississippi and surrounding regions.
