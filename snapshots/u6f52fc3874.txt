APPLY FOR AID
The Iowa Trans Mutual Aid fund applications are open during the first week of the month. The process of receiving aid spans one month. As a reminder, the ITMAF provides aid for gender-affirming care for Trans, Nonbinary, and Gender Diverse Iowans. More details are included in the application link (above).
If you have any questions about applying, please reach out to the ITMAF at itmaf@proton.me
