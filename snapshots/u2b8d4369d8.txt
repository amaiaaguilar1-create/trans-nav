SECTION 1. DO NOT CODIFY. Legislative intent.25
It is the intent of the General Assembly to:26
(1) Clarify and reconcile the meaning of "sex", "male", and27
"female" in state law; and28

rooms, and sleeping quarters in facilities where women have traditionally30
