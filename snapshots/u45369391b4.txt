Legal Assistance
Please read the guide below before submitting your request.
At Gender Justice, we take cases that move the law forward. Our expertise centers primarily on gender inequality. If your situation does not relate to an issue involving gender inequality or does not present an opportunity to move the law forward, we may be unable to advise you and will instead direct you to another resource.
If you are unsure whether your case fits these guidelines, please fill out our intake form so we can help you evaluate whether Gender Justice is a good fit for your case. Please note that we are not a referral agency and may not be able to provide a specific referral in your case.
