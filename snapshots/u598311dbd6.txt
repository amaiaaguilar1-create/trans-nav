Portland Regional Office
The Portland Regional Office of Oregon Law Center serves low-income clients in the counties of Clackamas,Crook, Hood River, Deschutes, Jefferson, Multnomah, Sherman, Wasco. We primarily accept cases referred by our legal aid partner offices, Legal Aid Services of Oregon’s Portland Regional Office and Central Oregon Regional Office, and cases referred by community-based organizations. To request legal assistance, please call the legal aid office for your county. You can find your local office here . The cases we typically receive by referral are in the areas of employment, housing, consumer and mortgage fraud, and public benefits. We provide legal services in English, Spanish and other languages.
The Portland office has Spanish speaking staff available. The office does not provide assistance with family law matters. If you have a family law matter such as custody, divorce, and protective orders: contact LASO Bend if you live in Jefferson, Deschutes or Crook counties; contact LASO Portland for family law matters if you live in Multnomah, Clackamas, Hood River, Wasco, Sherman counties.
