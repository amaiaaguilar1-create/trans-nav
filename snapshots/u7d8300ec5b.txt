Name Change Information
Office Visit Required
To change your name on your license/ID card you must appear in person at the Driver License Division.
Documents Required
View list below of acceptable documents to bring with you to the office to change your name.
Translation
Foreign documents must be translated to English by a certified Translator .
**This page is for informational purposes only .**
If you need to schedule an appointment for a driver license or ID card, return to the previous page you were on, or the Homepage , and select Driver License Services or ID Card Services, and then select original (first time), renewal, or replacement for the license/ID type you need.
If you were recently married/divorced, or the name on your driver license or ID card doesn't match the name on your birth certificate, amended birth certificate, or passport, you'll need legal documentation to explain the difference.
These documents could be:
A certified marriage certificate from the state
A divorce decree
An updated passport
A new birth certificate
Adoption papers
A citizenship or naturalization certificate
Court papers showing the name change, sealed by the court
