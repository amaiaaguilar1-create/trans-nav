We understand your concern in these uncertain times but count on this: your health is our priority, and Planned Parenthood will never stop working to build a world where all people can get the care they need. Massachusetts remains a state that protects access to sexual and reproductive health care, including abortion and gender-affirming care. Our doors are open at Planned Parenthood League of Massachusetts with no changes in services. We have a strong team working to ensure you continue to have access to comprehensive, safe, and legal reproductive care.
In the event of changes that would require us to shift any of our services, we will send MyChart messages to current patients and update our website accordingly.
MyChart Patient Portal
All of our patients will be able to use a MyChart account to schedule appointments, track test results and keep in touch with our clinical staff. When you schedule your next appointment, you will be given instructions on how to access MyChart.
