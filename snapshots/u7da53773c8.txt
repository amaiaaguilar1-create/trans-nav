The Delaware State Courts will be closed on Wednesday, November 11, 2026 in observance of Veterans Day. Justice of the Peace Court 11 (New Castle County) and Justice of the Peace Court 7 (Kent County) will remain open 24 hours; Justice of the Peace Court 3 (Sussex County) will be open from 6AM – 12AM (no new cases after 11:30PM); and the Statewide Virtual Hearing Criminal Court operations will remain available.
