1. Findings
The Court having read and considered the Petition for Change of Name finds:
The statutory requirements for a name change under C.R.S. § 13-15-101, have been met.
The desired name change is proper and not detrimental to the interests of any other person.
The name change is in the best interest of the minor child.
The party changing names, being an adult or child 14 years of age or older, has not been
convicted of a felony or adjudicated a juvenile delinquent for an offense that would constitute
a felony if committed by an adult in this state or any other state or under federal law.
Publication is not required. The Petitioner has shown good cause why publication should not
apply or otherwise qualify under C.R.S. § 13-15-102.
