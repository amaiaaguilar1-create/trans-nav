There is no vaccine for HIV infection, but there are ways to prevent it. You can lower your chance of getting HIV by taking medicine called pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP). There are many reasons why people choose to take PrEP:
Your sex partner is HIV-positive.
You have more than one sexual partner.
You share injection drug equipment with others.
You want to be on PrEP to lower your risk of HIV infection.
Free PrEP
We want to increase access to pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) for everyone, regardless of their insurance status. Our goal is to reduce the number of new cases of HIV in Utah.
University of Utah Health offers PrEP for free if you do not have health insurance.
PrEP is covered by most health insurance plans. We can provide you with PrEP if your insurance is accepted by U of U Health . If we do not accept your health insurance, we can also connect you with a PrEP provider in our community who does.
We offer virtual and in-person PrEP appointments Monday–Friday.
