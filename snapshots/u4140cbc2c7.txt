Driver&#x27;s license forms
Form Use this form to:
​Application for ​a​ Driver&#x27;s License or Identification Card​ (PS33100) Apply for a Minnesota instruction permit, driver&#x27;s license or identification card.
​​ Application for a Driver&#x27;s License Fee Refund​ (PS31081) Request a refund of driver&#x27;s license fees you paid.
​ Application for Limited License for Child Support Suspension (​PS33030) Request a limited license for a child support suspension.​
​Autism Spectrum - Mental Health - Emergency/Caretaker Contacts​​ (PS34300) Add or remove emergency contact or caretaker information from your driver record. Add or remove the Autism Spectrum and/or Mental Health Indicator from your card.
​ Certificate of Translation (PS33210) Provide proof of document translation. This form must be completed in English.
​ Crash Victims Memorial Wall – Participation Release (PS32005) Participate in the Crash Victims Memorial Wall at DVS exam stations.
​​​Customer service recognition or complaint Submit feedback to DVS about a customer service experience.
​ Farm Work license (PS30188) ​​Request a restricted provisional license for a farm work.
​ Insurance Certification (​PS35016) ​Provide proof of auto insurance. Must be completed by authorized representative (not agent) of insurance company.
​ Last Use Statement (PS31083) ​Report the last time you consumed a drink or product containing alcohol or controlled substances.
​​License for Medical Reason Exemption (PS30188) Request a restricted provisional license for a medical reason.
​ Ophthalmologist Examination Report (​D4) Provide proof of examination for intrastate school bus drivers whose diabetic condition has had an effect on their visual health. This must be completed by an ophthalmologist.
​ Petition for a Variance - No ID or Residency Documents (PS33160) ​​Petition for a variance from providing necessary documents to get a license or ID.
​ Petition for a Variance - Religious Conflict with DL/ID Requirements (PS30371) ​​Petition for a variance from card photo requirements for religious reasons.
​​​Petition for a Variance - Homeboun​d Photo Variance​ (PS2907) ​
Petition for a variance from card photo requirements due to being homebound.
​ Driver&#x27;s License Private Data Request​ (PS33202​DL) Request your address on file with DVS be private due to a grave concern for your safety or the safety of your family.
​ Rehabilitation Requirements (PS31091) ​​Review rehabilitation requirements or provide supporting statement for someone requesting reinstatement.
​​ Request for Administrative Review (Implied Consent Cancelation or Revocation) (PS31122) ​​Request an administrative review if your driving privilege was revoked or disqualified under the implied consent law.
​ Request for Examination of Driver (​PS31924) Request reexamination of someone&#x27;s driving ability.
​ Restriction Removal (​PS2122) Request alcohol restriction on your license be removed.
Special Review Awareness (PS31086) ​​Acknowledge you have multiple alcohol or controlled substance offenses on your driving record.
Supervised Driving Log Record the hours you spend practicing driving.
Loss of Consciousness or Voluntary Control Waiver (PS31015) ​Report a loss of consciousness or voluntary control. Must be signed by physician.
​ Victim of Identity Theft (​PS31300) Add a law enforcement alert of identity theft to your record.
​ Vision Report (Visual Acuity) (​PS30338) Submit a vision report. Must be signed by licensed vision examiner.
Voluntary Surrender of Driving Privileges​ (PS33061A) Request your driving privileges be voluntarily surrendered.
