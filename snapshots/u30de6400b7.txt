Vital records in Missouri can be corrected or amended in specific instances in accordance with state statutes and regulations with the proper documentary evidence and/or legal documents 1 . Some of these changes can be performed with a correction affidavit and applicable documentation, while some changes or amendments, such as a legal name change , require a court order. If a court order is required, you will likely need a lawyer or, in some cases, you can represent yourself .
If you cannot afford legal services, and do not wish to represent yourself, you may be eligible for pro bono program services. Typically, any court order submitted to the Bureau of Vital Records (930 Wildwood Drive, Jefferson City, MO 65109) should be a certified copy and may require a statutory fee for processing.
For more information about requesting a correction or amendment to a Missouri vital record, see below or contact the Bureau of Vital Records at 573-751-6387, option 2.
