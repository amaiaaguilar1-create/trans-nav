About the Transgender Program
The Center's Transgender Program is a welcoming, affirming program for transgender and gender diverse individuals. We offer weekly and monthly social and support groups, events, and opportunities to connect with our community.
We also offer engaging classes, such as yoga or art workshops, host community discussions, and provide fingerprinting services on-site.
