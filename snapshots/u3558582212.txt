Our Purpose
Our mission is to empower and advocate for LGBTQIA+ people in Connecticut through community, health, and learning programs.
Our vision is to create a world where LGBTQIA+ individuals are celebrated, feel safe, and can access essential resources without barriers.

Please Note: At this time, Triangle Community Center’s Care Coordination and Brief Solutions-Focused Counseling programs are operating with a waiting list for new clients. We are continuing to review referrals and will reach out as capacity becomes available.
Please note that Care Coordination and Counseling are not crisis services and we may not be able to provide immediate support. If you are experiencing an emergency or are in immediate danger, call 911. If you are in crisis or need urgent mental health support, please call or text 988 or contact 211 Connecticut for additional local resources and referrals.
