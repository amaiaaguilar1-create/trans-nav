VLN LGBTQ Clinic
At this phone advice clinic, clients receive free 20-minute meetings with a volunteer attorney to answer legal questions, review legal papers, fill out paperwork, or provide advice about court procedures regarding LGBTQ-related legal issues. Meeting topics include, but are not limited to, legal advice regarding name and gender marker changes for both minors and adults , and other LGBTQ-related issues.
Clients interested in scheduling an appointment for this clinic can fill out the online application linked in the Application Process notes.
