Change a Gender Marker on a Wisconsin Birth Certificate
From TRANS LAW HELP :
At this point in Wisconsin, only people who have had what the law describes as a &ldquo;surgical sex-change procedure&rdquo; may legally change their gender marker. See Wis. Stat. 69,15 (4)(b) .
You should discuss with your healthcare provider(s) whether, in their medical opinion, the gender affirming care you receive qualifies as a &ldquo;surgical sex-change procedure.&rdquo;
NOTE: Wisconsin requires either an F or M gender marker on birth certificates. There is no gender neutral or X option.
