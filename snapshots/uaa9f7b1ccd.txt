Certificate Requests
Page Content
​ ​​​​​​​​​​The Vital Registration Office provides certified copies of births, deaths, marriages, and births resulting in stillbirth. The fastest way to obtain a certified copy of a vital record is to order in-person for same day service.
Please note fee change effective July 1, 2023.&#160; Click here for a copy of the fee change memo .
In Person
​
Ordering in person is the fas​test way to obtain a certificate.&#160;Same-day walk in service is available&#160;at the Vital Registration Office at 350 Capitol Street in Charleston.&#160;A&#160;walk up counter is on the right&#160;side of the main lobby.&#160;Please allow at least a 15-minute wait time, even though most requests are completed much quicker.&#160; Keep&#160;in mind&#160;11&#58;30 am&#160;to 1&#58;00 pm&#160;is a busy time, so please allow for a&#160;longer wait time.&#160;The cost of a certificate is $12 per copy.&#160;The fee includes a non-refundable search fee. If a computerized search does not find the record, a search will be conducted for the year specified, the year before, and the year after. Additional searches may be paid for at a rate of $12 per three-year period. Accepted forms of payment are&#160;cash, check, and money order&#160;only. Debit/credit cards are accepted at the walk up counter and incur a 4% surcharge.&#160; To shorten your wait time, you can fill out the application form in advance and bring it with you to&#160;the office.&#160;Go to the&#160; Certificate Request Forms​ &#160;page and&#160;then choose the appropriate application form.​
To obtain a certified copy of a birth or death certificate, you must present one of the following forms of identification&#58;
State issued driver's license (can be expired)
United States, or Foreign passports
Military ID
Photo ID cards from a government sponsored program
Any two of the following forms of identification are accepted&#58;
Major credit card
Motor vehicle registration card
State social services program card
A recent utility bill in the name of the person who is requesting the certificate
If you do not have any of these forms of identification,&#160;exceptions can be granted on a case-by-case basis.
By Mail
The cost of a certificate is $12 per copy.&#160;The fee includes a non-refundable search fee. If a computerized search does not find the record, a search will be conducted for the year specified, the year before, and the year after. Additional searches may be paid for at a rate of $12 per three year period. Accepted forms of payment are&#160;check and money order&#160;only. Debit/credit cards by mail are not ac​cepted. To order a certified copy of a birth or death certificate, fill out the application form and mail to the Vital Registration Office with a color copy of your identification (see above)&#160;and a&#160;check or money order for the correct fee at the address below.&#160; Go to the Certificate Request Forms​ &#160;page&#160;and then choose the appropriate application form.&#160;The applica​tion form, a copy of identification as listed above, and proper payment is required for all mail requests.
