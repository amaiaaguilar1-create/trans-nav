Objective: This policy describes the required fees for issuance of a driver’s license and/or
identification card and duration of credentials based on type and class.
Definitions:
• Application Fee – A fee assessed on a commercial driver’s license (CDL) for processing the
application for issuance. This fee is charged for each application.
• Duplicate Driver’s License – The re-issuance of a current, Louisiana driver’s license that is
not within ninety (90) days of the expiration date. The expiration date of the license will not
change using a duplicate transaction.
• Duplicate Identification Card – The re-issuance of a current, Louisiana identification card, in
which the expiration date does not change.
General:
• Duration of the credential (i.e. driver’s license or identification card) is based on type and class,
as described in the chart below.
Required Documentation:
• Required documentation is outlined in the applicable policy for credential type and/or class.
Fees:
• Fees for all credentials are determined by the type and class, as shown in the tables below.
• When upgrading and the applicant does not have the credential in hand, they are not to be
charged a duplicate fee before processing the upgrade (i.e. changing from a temporary
instructional permit to permit)
• A $15.00 delinquent fee is required in addition to all other fees, if the driver’s license is
expired more than ten (10) calendar days.
o Exception - There is no delinquent fee for applicants seventy (70) years old or older or
non-permanent residents.
