Awareness Means Showing Up for Our Community: Transgender Awareness Week
November 11, 2026
Transgender , LGBTQ+ History , LGBTQ+ Holidays , TAW & TDoR
While Transgender Awareness Week or TAW (November 13-19) is an opportunity to educate the public on transgender issues, stories, and their contributions to our communities while addressing the barriers that affect their lives, Transgender Day of Remembrance or TDoR (November 20) is a time to honor and reflect on the lives lost to anti-transgender violence.
At OUTMemphis we believe that every person deserves access to affirming care and resources, meaningful connections, and support that meets them where they are.
