WELCOME
The Gender Health Center (GHC) is a trans and Black, Indigenous, and People of Color-led non-profit organization located in Sacramento, CA dedicated to the health, wellness, and joy of transgender, non-binary and gender expansive communities to ensure health equity for all.
​
GHC offers a wide range of free or low-cost, low-barrier services to all community, including a free health clinic, HIV testing and linkage to care, mental health counseling, support groups and cultural programs, harm reduction, legal assistance, healthcare navigation, case management, resource referrals, and education and training.
