How We Can Help
Some of the ID Services we offer:
Technical Assistance:
Trouble-shooting – what is the fastest and most efficient route to get ID
Applying for Social Security Card, Birth Certificate and PennDOT photo ID on behalf of someone without ID
Assistance collecting documents from schools, medical providers, etc.
Referring clients for legal name changes to VIP if/when necessary
Providing clients with verification letters stating that CLS is working with them to get their identity documents and that we can verify his/her identity.
Advocacy:
Advocating with Social Security, Vital Records and PennDOT on behalf of clients
Advocating for clients who need ID for their probation/parole officer, the welfare office, SSA, etc.
What to Bring
Before you call, please gather any identity documents you do have and any communications you may have received regarding your identity document issue.
