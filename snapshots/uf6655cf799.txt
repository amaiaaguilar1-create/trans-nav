Consumer Services
IDOI Consumer Services Department is here to inform and protect consumers from illegal insurance practices by ensuring insurance companies and licensed producers operating in Indiana comply with State insurance laws. We can assist you with your insurance inquiry, or complaint, about health, life, automobile, and property and casualty insurance policies.
To ask an insurance question:
Call 800-622-4461 or 317-232-2395
Email consumerservices@idoi.in.gov
