We're Here
The Newark LGBTQ Community Center is a volunteer-run, community-based organization committed to providing safe space, programs, and services that support the well-being of the LGBTQ community and our allies in the Newark, New Jersey area.
OUR MISSION:
We provide support, advocacy, and a safe, inclusive space for the Newark LGBTQ+ community.
OUR VISION:
We envision a Newark where the LGBTQ+ community is visible, thriving, and celebrated.
Our offices are located inside Equal Space at
89 Market St., 2nd Floor, Newark, New Jersey 07102. Physical mail can be sent to:
PO BOX - 200434 Newark, NJ 07102.
