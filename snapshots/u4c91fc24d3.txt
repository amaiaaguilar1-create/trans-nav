Is Online Gambling Legal in Florida? Chapter 849 and Amendment 3
People ask whether online gambling in Florida is legal because the answer is layered. Chapter 849 of the Florida Statutes restricts most gambling activity. Amendment 3, added to the state constitution as Article X, Section 30, requires voter approval for expansion of casino gambling, with exceptions for tribal gaming under compacts. The Florida Gaming Control Commission (FGCC) oversees gambling enforcement.
The Tribal Gaming Compact with the Seminole Tribe covers retail casinos and sports betting, including online sports wagering through Hard Rock Bet. It does not grant licences to other online casino operators. Legislators have floated proposals concerning internet gambling, but no commercial online casino licensing framework exists here. Whether any change occurs depends on future law.
The age threshold is 21 for casino gambling and sports betting, and 18 for poker rooms, the lottery and pari-mutuel wagering. The sites on this page are not licensed by Florida. Treat offshore licensing as a different level of protection, and consider consulting a legal professional about your situation.
