LSU-CrescentCare Sexual Health Center
Services
Your local home for sexual wellness and peace of mind
At CrescentCare, we create partnerships that benefit the wellness of our communities. Our partnership with LSU Health and the creation of the Sexual Health Center has allowed us to provide peace of mind to anyone seeking same-day testing and treatment for sexually transmitted infections.
Rapid testing for HIV and syphilis with same-day results
Clinicians on-site for same day diagnosis and treatment of STIs and vaginitis.
Most medications provided onsite for common STIs
Walk-ins accepted, same-day and pre-scheduled appointments also available
The Sexual Health Center is located at CrescentCare’s MidCity location at 2515 Canal Street.
Hours of Operation: Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. – 3 p.m.
We offer healthcare services for all! Ask about our sliding-scale fee service. If you are uninsured, please don’t hesitate to visit our location for services. We are happy to test and treat you.
