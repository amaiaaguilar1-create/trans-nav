Overview
DPHSS Clinics provide accessible healthcare services that support preventive care, routine medical services, chronic disease management, and community wellness initiatives.
Clinic Services
General medical consultations
Preventive healthcare screenings
Immunizations and vaccinations
Maternal and child health services
Chronic disease management
Health education and counseling
Laboratory services and referrals
Wellness assessments
Goals of Clinic Services
Improve access to healthcare
Promote preventive medicine
Support disease prevention and management
Provide community-based healthcare services
Improve overall public health outcomes
Importance to the Community
Clinics serve as critical healthcare access points for individuals and families, particularly underserved and vulnerable populations.
